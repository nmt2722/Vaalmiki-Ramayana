Ways to read

Every page is built to be read at the pace of a shloka, not a scroll.

Three scripts, one verse

Devanagari for recitation, IAST for pronunciation, and a plain English translation — always together. Open the word-by-word panel when you want to see how a line is built.

Follow the story

Kanda overviews give the arc; each sarga page keeps the previous and next sarga within reach, so the epic can be read continuously rather than searched.

Meet the characters

The character index records where each figure first appears and how often they return, with links straight into the verse where the story introduces them.

Choose a reading path

If you are new to the Ramayana, these five doorways open the whole house.

  • Bala Kanda 1The very beginningNarada arrives, and Valmiki begins to ask.
  • Ayodhya Kanda 1The exileAyodhya prepares for a coronation that never comes.
  • Sundara Kanda 1The heart of the epicHanuman stands on the southern shore and leaps.
  • Yuddha Kanda 1The war beginsThe armies gather; the ocean waits for a bridge.
  • Yuddha Kanda 128The coronationRama returns, and Ayodhya lights every lamp.
