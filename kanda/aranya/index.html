Kanda III

अरण्यकाण्ड

Aranya Kanda

The Book of the Forest

The forest years. Rama, Sita and Lakshmana live among sages and demons, moving from hermitage to hermitage. Shurpanakha's humiliation brings the rakshasa armies of Khara; then comes Ravana's golden deer, the abduction of Sita, and the death of the vulture-king Jatayu.
