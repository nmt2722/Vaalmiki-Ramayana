Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 33

An ogre named Sarama reassures Seetha by telling her that Ravana…

An ogre named Sarama reassures Seetha by telling her that Ravana, the conjurer created an illusory appearance of Rama's head and exhibited it before her.

1Yuddha Kanda 33.1

सीताम् तु मोहिताम् दृष्ट्वा सरमा नाम राक्षसी |
आससाद आशु वैदेहीम् प्रियाम् प्रणयिनी सखी

sītām tu mohitām dṛṣṭvā saramā nāma rākṣasī |
āsasāda āśu vaidehīm priyām praṇayinī sakhī

Seeing the bewildered Seetha, a demon named Sarama*, an affectionate female
companion then approached Seetha, her beloved Seetha, her beloved friend.

9Yuddha Kanda 33.9

11Yuddha Kanda 33.11

An ogre named Sarama reassures Seetha by telling her that Ravana, the conjurer created an illusory appearance of Rama's head and exhibited it before her. She informs Seetha that Rama is still alive and is stationed along with his forces at the southern shore of the ocean. Sarama restores confidence to Seetha, by saying that Rama after killing Ravana in battle will soon win her back.
