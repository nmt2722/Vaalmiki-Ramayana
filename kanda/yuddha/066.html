Seeing Kumbhakarna coming with his colossal body…

Seeing Kumbhakarna coming with his colossal body and emitting a tremendous roar, the monkeys scare away.

1Yuddha Kanda 66.1

स लङ्घायित्वा प्राकारम् गिरिकूटोपमो महान् |
निर्ययौ निगरात्तूत्णम् कुम्भकर्णो महाबलः

2Yuddha Kanda 66.2

3Yuddha Kanda 66.3

तमवध्यं मघवता यमेन वरुणेन च |

5Yuddha Kanda 66.5

6Yuddha Kanda 66.6

7Yuddha Kanda 66.7

8Yuddha Kanda 66.8

9Yuddha Kanda 66.9

10Yuddha Kanda 66.10

11Yuddha Kanda 66.11

12Yuddha Kanda 66.12

Rishabha, Sharabha, Mainda, Dhumra, Neela, Kumuda, sushena, Gavaksha, Rambha, Tara
and more particularly Dvipada, Panasa and hanuma marched ahead very quickly, with their faces
turned towards the battle.

Seeing Kumbhakarna coming with his colossal body and emitting a tremendous roar, the monkeys scare away. Angada reassures the monkeys, who then return to the battle field to resume fight. When the monkeys start attacking Kumbhakarna, the latter crushes some monkeys with rage. Then again, the monkeys get frightened and run away in different directions. Angada then restores the monkeys to confidence once more and all the monkeys stand awaiting the command of Angada.
