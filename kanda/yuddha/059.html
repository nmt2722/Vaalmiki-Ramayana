Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 59

Feeling desperate at the death of Prahasta, Ravana himself appears…

Feeling desperate at the death of Prahasta, Ravana himself appears on the battle-front for an encounter, along with Akampana, Indrajit, Atikaya, Mahodara, Pishacha, Trishiras, Kumbha, Nimkumbha and Narantaka the foremost of demons.
