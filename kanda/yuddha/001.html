Rama appreciates Hanuma and embraces him after hearing his report.Rama was gladdend that Hanuma did something worthy of a noble servent. Then, Rama reflected upon the problem of how to cross the ocean.

1Yuddha Kanda 1.1

श्रुत्वा हनुमतो वाक्यम् यथावद्भिभाषितम् |
रामः प्रीतिसमायुक्तो वाक्यमुत्तरमब्रवीत्

2Yuddha Kanda 1.2

3Yuddha Kanda 1.3

4Yuddha Kanda 1.4

5Yuddha Kanda 1.5

को विशेत्सुदुराधर्षाम् राक्षसैश्च सुरक्षिताम्

"Who is able to capture that citadel, by assault, that is exceedingly dangerous to

6Yuddha Kanda 1.6

7Yuddha Kanda 1.7

कुर्यात्तदुनुरागेण तमहुः पुरुषोत्तमम्

8Yuddha Kanda 1.8

9Yuddha Kanda 1.9

10Yuddha Kanda 1.10

11Yuddha Kanda 1.11

अहम् च रघवंशश्च लक्ष्मणश्च महाबलः

12Yuddha Kanda 1.12

13Yuddha Kanda 1.13

14Yuddha Kanda 1.14
