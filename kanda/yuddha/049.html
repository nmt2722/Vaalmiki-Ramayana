1Yuddha Kanda 49.1

घोरेण शर बन्धेन बद्धौ दशरथ आत्मजौ |
निश्वसन्तौ यथा नागौ शयानौ रुधिर उक्षितौ

ghoreṇa śara bandhena baddhau daśaratha ātmajau |

strong, including Sugreeva, plunged in grief, were standing around those two illustrious
warriors.

28Yuddha Kanda 49.28

न च अतिक्रमितुम् शक्यम् दैवम् सुग्रीव मानुषैः |

30Yuddha Kanda 49.30

शुश्रुवुस् तस्य ते सर्वे वानराह् परिदेवितम्

32Yuddha Kanda 49.32

तम् दृष्ट्वा त्वरितम् यान्तम् नील अन्जन चय उपमम्
वानरा दुद्रुवुह् सर्वे मन्यमानास् तु रावणिम्

tam dṛṣṭvā tvaritam yāntam nīla anjana caya upamam
vānarā dudruvuh sarve manyamānās tu rāvaṇim

Seeing him, who resembled a mass of collyrium, hastening towards them thus, all the
monkeys thinking him to be Indrajit the son of Ravana, fled away.

the son of Ravana;dudruvuH= fled away.

Returning to consciousness, Rama laments over the plight of Lakshmana, who was lying unconscious. The foremost of monkeys too were plunged in grief and despondency on seeing Rama lamenting as aforesaid. In the meantime, Vibhishana approaches Rama and the monkeys flee away, mistaking him as Indrajit.
