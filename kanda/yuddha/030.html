Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 30

Dispatched by the king Ravana to ascertain the strength of the army…

Dispatched by the king Ravana to ascertain the strength of the army in the opposition-camp, Shardula the demon-spy acquaints his master with the important leaders of the monkeys.

The two divinities of Ashvin are said to appear in the sky before the dawn in a
golden carriage drawn by horses or birds, they bring treasures to men and avert misfortune and
