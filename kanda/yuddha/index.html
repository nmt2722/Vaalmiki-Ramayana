Kanda VI

युद्धकाण्ड

Yuddha Kanda

The Book of War

The war. The ocean is bridged, Lanka is besieged, and the great rakshasa warriors fall one by one. Sita's fire ordeal ends the war, and Rama returns to Ayodhya for his coronation. The six kandas close with the Ramarajya, the reign of dharma.
