Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 23

Rama explains to Lakshmana the various portents, he observes around…

Rama explains to Lakshmana the various portents, he observes around him, that signal a destructive war fare. He orders for formation of battalions in the army and surges forth together with the battalions towards Lanka.

1Yuddha Kanda 23.1

निमित्तानि निमित्तज्ञो दृष्ट्वा लक्ष्मणपूर्वजः |
सौमित्रिम् सम्परिष्वज्य इदम् वचनम्ब्रवीत्

nimittāni nimittajño dṛṣṭvā lakṣmaṇapūrvajaḥ |
saumitrim sampariṣvajya idam vacanambravīt

Rama, who was well acquainted with portents, on seeing the portents around, embraced
Lakshmana and spoke as follows:

1. lakshhmaNa puurvajaH= Rama;nimittaj^NaH= who was acquainted with omens;dR^IshhTNaa= on seeing;nimittaani= the omens;samparishhvajya= embraced;soumitrim= Lakshmana;abraviit= and spoke;idam= these;vachanam= words.

2Yuddha Kanda 23.2

परिगृह्योदकम् शीतम् वनानि फलवन्ति च |
बलौ घम् सम्विभज्येमम् व्यूह्य तिष्ठेम लक्ष्मण

parigṛhyodakam śītam vanāni phalavanti ca |
balau gham samvibhajyemam vyūhya tiṣṭhema lakṣmaṇa

"O, Lakshmana! Acquiring this region endowed with cold water and woods abounding in
fruits, let us speedily divide this multitude of forces into battalions and drawing it up in battle
array, we shall stand attentive."

Lakshmana;parigR^ihya= acquiring (this region provided with);shiitam= cold;udakam= water;vanaanicha= and woods;phalavanti= abounding in fruit;samvibhajya= let us speedily divide;idam= this;balougham= multitude of forces;
(into battalions);vyuuhya= and drawing it up in battle array;tishhThema= we shall remain stand at attention.

3Yuddha Kanda 23.3

लोकक्षयकरम् भीमम् भयम् पश्याम्युपस्थितम् |
प्रबर्हणम् प्रवीराणामृक्षवानररक्षसाम्

lokakṣayakaram bhīmam bhayam paśyāmyupasthitam |
prabarhaṇam pravīrāṇāmṛkṣavānararakṣasām

"I perceive an impending danger which will be terrific, causing destruction to the

4Yuddha Kanda 23.4

वाताश्च कलुषा वान्ति कम्पते च वसुन्धरा |
पर्वताग्राणि वेपन्ते पतन्ति च महीरुःआः

vātāśca kaluṣā vānti kampate ca vasundharā |
parvatāgrāṇi vepante patanti ca mahīruḥāḥ

"Winds are blowing with dust and earth is trembling. Mountain- tops are quivering

5Yuddha Kanda 23.5

मेघाः क्रव्यादसम्काशाः परुषाः परुषस्वनाः |
क्रूराः क्रूरम् प्रवर्षन्ति मिश्रम् शोणितबिन्दुभिः

meghāḥ kravyādasamkāśāḥ paruṣāḥ paruṣasvanāḥ |
krūrāḥ krūram pravarṣanti miśram śoṇitabindubhiḥ

"Ferocious clouds resembling wild beasts were dirty colored and emit a terrific

6Yuddha Kanda 23.6

रक्तचन्दनसम्काशा संध्या परमदारुणा |
ज्वलतः प्रपतत्येतदादित्यादग्निमण्डलम्

raktacandanasamkāśā saṃdhyā paramadāruṇā |
jvalataḥ prapatatyetadādityādagnimaṇḍalam

"Evening twilight resembling red sandal wood is very much dreadful. From the blazing

7Yuddha Kanda 23.7

दीना दीनस्वराः क्रूराः सर्वतो मृगपक्षिणः |
प्रत्यादित्यम् विनर्दन्ति जनयन्तो महद्भयम्

dīnā dīnasvarāḥ krūrāḥ sarvato mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ |
pratyādityam vinardanti janayanto mahadbhayam

"Wild animals and birds from all sides are roaring pitiably with melancholic sound,

8Yuddha Kanda 23.8

रजन्यामप्रकाशस्तु सम्तापयति चन्द्रमाः |
कृष्णरक्तांशुपर्यन्तो लोकक्षय इवोदितः

rajanyāmaprakāśastu samtāpayati candramāḥ |
kṛṣṇaraktāṃśuparyanto lokakṣaya ivoditaḥ

"The splendorous moon as though rising at the time of universal dissolution,

9Yuddha Kanda 23.9

ह्रस्वो रूक्षोऽप्रशस्तश्च परिवेषस्तु लोहितः |
आदित्ये विमले वीलम् लक्ष्म लक्ष्मण दृश्यते

hrasvo rūkṣo.apraśastaśca pariveṣastu lohitaḥ |
āditye vimale vīlam lakṣma lakṣmaṇa dṛśyate

"O, Lakshmana! A dark stain appears on the cloudless solar disc, which is
diminished, dreary, inauspicious and coppery."

9. lakshhmaNa= O;Lakshmana! Niilam= a dark;lakshhma= stain;dR^ishyate= appears;vimale aaditye pariveshhaH= on the colorless solar disc;hrasvaH= which is diminished;ruukshhaH= dreary;aprashastah= inauspicious;lohitaH= and coppery.

10Yuddha Kanda 23.10

रजसा महता चापि नक्षत्राणि हतानि च |
युगान्तमिव लोकानाम् पश्य शसन्ति लक्ष्मण

rajasā mahatā cāpi nakṣatrāṇi hatāni ca |
yugāntamiva lokānām paśya śasanti lakṣmaṇa

"O, Lakshmana! Look! Stars enveloped in enormous dust, appear to announce a

11Yuddha Kanda 23.11

काकाः श्येनास्तथा नीचा गृध्राः परिपतन्ति च |
शिवाश्चाप्यशुभान्नादान्नदन्ति सुमहाभयान्

kākāḥ śyenāstathā nīcā gṛdhrāḥ paripatanti ca |
śivāścāpyaśubhānnādānnadanti sumahābhayān

"Crows, eagles and vultures are flying low. Jackals too are howling very dreadful

12Yuddha Kanda 23.12

13Yuddha Kanda 23.13

क्षिप्रमद्यैव दुर्धर्षाम् पुरीम् रावणपालिताम् |

"Abounding with all the monkeys we shall attack the city ruled by Ravana, now itself

14Yuddha Kanda 23.14

इत्येवमुक्त्वा धन्वी स रामः सम्ग्रामधर्षणः |
प्रतस्थे पुरतो रामो लङ्कामभिमुखो विभुः

ityevamuktvā dhanvī sa rāmaḥ samgrāmadharṣaṇaḥ |
pratasthe purato rāmo laṅkāmabhimukho vibhuḥ

Rama the lord, the conqueror of enemies and a charming man thus speaking, wielded a
bow and sallied forth in front, facing towards Lanka.

15Yuddha Kanda 23.15

सविभीषणसुग्रीवाः सर्वे ते वानरर्षभाः |
प्रतस्थिरे विनर्दन्तो धृतानाम् द्विषताम् वधे

16Yuddha Kanda 23.16

राघवस्य प्रियार्थम् तु सुतराम् वीर्यशालिनाम् |
हरीणाम् कर्मचेष्टाभिस्तुतोष रघुनन्दनः
