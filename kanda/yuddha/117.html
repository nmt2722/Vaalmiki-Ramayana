Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 117

Gods reach Lanka in aerial cars and approach Rama

Gods reach Lanka in aerial cars and approach Rama. They ask Rama why he ignores Seetha, when she is entering into the fire. Rama appeals them to describe in reality who he is. Brahma (the creator) proclaims his divinity and eulogizes him with hymns.

25Yuddha Kanda 117.25

निमेषस्ते स्मृता रात्रिरुन्मेषो दिवसस्तथा
संस्कारास्त्वभवन्वेदा नैतदस्ति त्वया विना

nimeṣaste smṛtā rātrirunmeṣo divasastathā

26Yuddha Kanda 117.26

जगत्सर्वं शरीरं ते स्थैर्यं ते वसुधातलम्
अग्निः कोपः प्रसादस्ते सोमः श्रीवत्सलक्षणः

27Yuddha Kanda 117.27

त्वया लोकास्त्रयः क्रान्ताः पुरा स्वैर्विक्रमैस्त्रिभिः

28Yuddha Kanda 117.28

सीता लक्ष्मीर्भवान् विष्णुर्देवः कृष्णः प्रजापतिः
वधार्थं रावणस्येह प्रविष्टो मानुषीं तनुम्

sītā lakṣmīrbhavān viṣṇurdevaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ prajāpatiḥ

29Yuddha Kanda 117.29

30Yuddha Kanda 117.30

31Yuddha Kanda 117.31

अमोघास्ते भविष्यन्ति भक्तिमन्तो नरा भुवि

33Yuddha Kanda 117.33

इममार्षम् स्तवं दिव्यमितिहासं पुरातनम्
ये नराः कीर्तयिष्यन्ति नास्ति तेषां पराभवः

imamārṣam stavaṃ divyamitihāsaṃ purātanam
ye narāḥ kīrtayiṣyanti nāsti teṣāṃ parābhavaḥ

"Humiliation will never be the plight of those humans who will recite this hymn in a
divine ancient history, sung by Brahma, the foremost seer."

33. paraabhavaH= Humiliation;naasti= will never be the plight;teShaam ye naraaH= of those humans;kiirtayiShyanti= who will recite;imam stavam= this hymn;divyam= divine;puraatanam= ancient;itihaasam= history;aarSham= sung by Brahma;
the foremost seer.

Gods reach Lanka in aerial cars and approach Rama. They ask Rama why he ignores Seetha, when she is entering into the fire. Rama appeals them to describe in reality who he is. Brahma (the creator) proclaims his divinity and eulogizes him with hymns.
