Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 3

On hearing the words of Sugreeva, Rama requests Hanuma to describe Lanka…

On hearing the words of Sugreeva, Rama requests Hanuma to describe Lanka in detail. Hanuma gives a detailed description of Lanka as he saw it.

2Yuddha Kanda 3.2

3Yuddha Kanda 3.3

4Yuddha Kanda 3.4

बलस्य परिमाणम् च द्वार दुर्ग क्रियाम् अपि |

"You had the opportunity to catch a glimpse of the size of the army, the

7Yuddha Kanda 3.7

8Yuddha Kanda 3.8

राक्षसाश्च यथा स्निग्धा रावणस्य च तेजसा |
पराम् समृद्धिम् लंकायाः सागरस्य च भीमताम्

as follows.

रथिनः च अश्व वाहाः च कुल पुत्राः सुपूजिताः

nyarbudam rakṣasām atra uttara dvāram āśritam |
rathinaḥ ca aśva vāhāḥ ca kula putrāḥ supūjitāḥ

"A hundred millions of ogres, who are mounted in chariots or who ride on hoses, sons
of distinguished families and greatly honored, are positioned there at the northern gate."

27. nyarbudam= a hundred millions;rakshasaam= of ogres;atra= there;aashritam= are positioned;uttaradvaaram= at the northern gate;rathinaH= (they are) car-warriors;ashvavaahaashcha= horse-soldiers;kulaputraaH= sons of noble families;supuujitaaH= and greatly honored."

28Yuddha Kanda 3.28

शतम् शत सहस्राणाम् मध्यमम् गुल्मम् आश्रितम् |
यातु धाना दुराधर्षाः साग्र कोटिः च रक्षसाम्

śatam śata sahasrāṇām madhyamam gulmam āśritam |
yātu dhānā durādharṣāḥ sāgra koṭiḥ ca rakṣasām

"Moreover, ogres hundreds of thousands in number, difficult to attack, aggregating
to one and quarter of a crore of ogres are positioned at the central division."

28. atha= moreover;yaatudhaanaaH= ogres;shatashaH sahasraaNi= hundreds of thousands in number;duraadarshhaaH= difficult to assail;saagrakoTishcha= aggregating to one and quarter of a crore;rakshasaam= of ogres;aashritaaH= are positioned;madhyamaskandham= at the central division."

29Yuddha Kanda 3.29

ते मया सम्क्रमा भग्नाः परिखाः च अवपूरिताः |
दग्धा च नगरी लंका प्राकाराः च अवसादिताः
बलैकदेशः क्षपितो राक्षसानाम् महात्मनाम्

te mayā samkramā bhagnāḥ parikhāḥ ca avapūritāḥ |
dagdhā ca nagarī laṃkā prākārāḥ ca avasāditāḥ
balaikadeśaḥ kṣapito rākṣasānām mahātmanām

"Those bridges were broken by me and the moats were filled up (with the wreckage).
The city of Lanka was burnt by me and the defensive walls were pulled down. A part of the army of
the gigantic ogres was destroyed."

29. te= those;samkramaaH= bridges;bhagnaaH= were broken;mayaa= by me;parikhaashcha= moats also;avapuuritaaH= filled up (with the wreckage);laN^kaa nagariim= the city of Lanka;daghaacha= was burnt;praakaaraashcha= defensive walls;avasaaditaaH= pulled down;balaikadeshaH= a part of the army;mahaatmanaam= of the gigantic;raakshasaanaam= ogres;kshapitaH= were destroyed.

30Yuddha Kanda 3.30

येन केन तु मार्गेण तराम वरुण आलयम्
हता इति नगरी लंकाम् वानरैर् अवधार्यताम्

yena kena tu mārgeṇa tarāma varuṇa ālayam
hatā iti nagarī laṃkām vānarair avadhāryatām

"By some way or other, let us cross the ocean. The city of Lanka should be assumed

let us cross the ocean. The city of Lanka should be assumed then as destroyed by the monkeys."

31Yuddha Kanda 3.31

अङ्गदो द्विविदो मैन्दो जाम्बवान् पनसो नलः

aṅgado dvivido maindo jāmbavān panaso nalaḥ

"Oh, Rama! What is the use for the rest of the forces to you? Angada, Divida,
Mainda, Jambavan, Panasa, Anala and Nila the commander-in-chief alone, by reaching that great city
of Ravana leaping and storming Lanka with its mountains and woods, moats and archways, protective
walls and buildings, will recover Seetha."

31;32;33. raaghava= Oh;Rama! kim= what is the use;balasheshhaNa= for the rest of the forces;tava= to you? aN^gadaH = Angada;dividaH= Divida;maindaH= Mainda;analaH= Anala;niilashchaiva= and Nila alone;senaapatiH= the commander-in-chief;gatvaa= by reaching;taam= that;raavaNasya mahaapuriim= great city of Ravana;plavamaanaaH= leaping;bhitvaa= and storming (it) saparvatavanaam = with its mountains and woods;sakhaataam= with moats;satoraNaam= with arch-ways;sapraakaaraam= with protective walls;sabhavanaam= with buildings;aanayishhyanti= and recover (Seetha).

34Yuddha Kanda 3.34

एवम् आज्ञापय क्षिप्रम् बलानाम् सर्व सम्ग्रहम् |
मुहूर्तेन तु युक्तेन प्रस्थानमभिरोचय

evam ājñāpaya kṣipram balānām sarva samgraham |
muhūrtena tu yuktena prasthānamabhirocaya

"Order quickly to get all the forces accumulated accordingly. Let us set out at the
appropriate moment."

34. aaJNaapaya= order;kshhipram= quickly;balaanaam sarvasangraham= to get all the forces;evam= accordingly;abhirochaya= be inclined;prasthaanam= for the march;yuktena= at the appropriate moment.

On hearing the words of Sugreeva, Rama requests Hanuma to describe Lanka in detail. Hanuma gives a detailed description of Lanka as he saw it.
