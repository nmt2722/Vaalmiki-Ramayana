Seetha gives a taunting reply to Rama, saying that why she was not abandoned by the latter, even at the time when Hanuma came to see her in Lanka.

1Yuddha Kanda 116.1

एवमुक्ता तु वैदेही परुषं लोमहर्षणम् |
राघवेण सरोषेण भृशं प्रव्यथिताभवत्

10Yuddha Kanda 116.10

सहसंवृद्धभावाच्च संसर्गेण च मानद |

11Yuddha Kanda 116.11

"As I have never been unfaithful in act, thought and speech to Rama, who knows all
the virtues, so let the fire-god protect me."

28Yuddha Kanda 116.28

आदित्योभवान् वायुर्दिशश्चन्द्रस्तथैव च |
अहश्चापि तथा सन्ध्ये रात्रिश्च पृथिवी तथा
यथान्येऽपि विजानन्ति तथा चारित्रसंयुताम्

ādityobhavān vāyurdiśaścandrastathaiva ca |
ahaścāpi tathā sandhye rātriśca pṛthivī tathā
yathānye.api vijānanti tathā cāritrasaṃyutām

"Since the adorable sun-god, wind-god, the four quarters and even so the moon-god,

even others know me to be endowed with good conduct, so let the fire-god protect me."

(let the fire-god protect me).

29Yuddha Kanda 116.29

एवमुक्त्वा तु वैदेही परिक्रम्य हुताशनम्
विवेश ज्वलनं दीप्तं निःसङ्गेनान्तरात्मना

evamuktvā tu vaidehī parikramya hutāśanam
viveśa jvalanaṃ dīptaṃ niḥsaṅgenāntarātmanā

Thus speaking, Seetha walking around the fire-god, with her mind free from
hesitation, entered the blazing fire.

30Yuddha Kanda 116.30

जनः स सुमहांस्तत्र बालवृद्धसमाकुलः
ददर्श मैथिलीं तत्र प्रविशन्तीं हुताशनम्

janaḥ sa sumahāṃstatra bālavṛddhasamākulaḥ

31Yuddha Kanda 116.31

सा तप्तनवहेमाभा तप्तकाञ्चनभूषणा
पपात ज्वलनं दीप्तं सर्वलोकस्य संनिधौ

sā taptanavahemābhā taptakāñcanabhūṣaṇā
papāta jvalanaṃ dīptaṃ sarvalokasya saṃnidhau

32Yuddha Kanda 116.32

ददृशुस्तां विशालाक्षीं पतन्तीं हव्यवाहनम्
सीतां सर्वाणि रूपाणि रुक्मवेदिनिभां तदा

33Yuddha Kanda 116.33

ददृशुस्तां महाभागां प्रविशन्तीं हुताशनम्
सीतां कृत्स्नास्त्रयो लोकाः पुण्यामाज्याहुतीमिव

dadṛśustāṃ mahābhāgāṃ praviśantīṃ hutāśanam

34Yuddha Kanda 116.34

प्रचुक्रुशुः स्त्रियः सर्वास्तां दृष्ट्वा हव्यवाहने
पतन्तीं संस्कृतां मन्रैर्वसोर्धारामिवाध्वरे

pracukruśuḥ striyaḥ sarvāstāṃ dṛṣṭvā havyavāhane

35Yuddha Kanda 116.35

ददृशुस्तां त्रयो लोका देवगन्धर्वदाननाः
शप्तां पतन्तीं निरये त्रिदिवाद्देवतामिव

36Yuddha Kanda 116.36

तस्यामग्निं विशन्त्यां तु हाहेति विपुलः स्वनः
रक्षसां वानराणां च सम्बभूवाद्भुतोपमः

tasyāmagniṃ viśantyāṃ tu hāheti vipulaḥ svanaḥ
rakṣasāṃ vānarāṇāṃ ca sambabhūvādbhutopamaḥ

While she was entering deeply into the fire, a loud sound, which appeared strange,
uttering 'Alas, Alas' rose both from the demons and the monkeys alike.

36. tasyaam= while she;vishantyaam= was entering deeply;agnim= into the fire;vipulaH svanaH= a loud sound;adbhutopamaH= which appeared strange;haa haa iti= uttering 'Alas;
Alas';sambabhuuva= rose;rakShasaam vaanaraaNaam cha= from both the demons and the monkeys alike.

Seetha gives a taunting reply to Rama, saying that why she was not abandoned by the latter, even at the time when Hanuma came to see her in Lanka. Then, Seetha requests Lakshmana to prepare a pile of fire for her to enter. When Lakshmana prepares a pyre, Seetha prays the fire-god and enters into it, in order toprove her conjugal fidelity.
