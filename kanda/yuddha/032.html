Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 32

Seetha was shocked, fell unconscious at the sight of Rama's head and bow

Seetha was shocked, fell unconscious at the sight of Rama's head and bow. She regained consciousness and asked Ravana to kill her also, so that she would join her husband in heaven.

1Yuddha Kanda 32.1

सा सीता तच्चिरो दृष्ट्वा तच् च कार्मुकम् उत्तमम् | |

sugrīva pratisaṃsargam ākhyātam ca hanūmatā

Seetha saw the illusory head and bow. She heard Ravana narrating about Rama's friendly
relationship with Sugreeva, as earlier apprised by Hanuman. Recognizing that head as that of Rama, with
a proof resembling her husband's eyes, facial complexion, hair, expanse of his forehead and the beautiful
jewel worn on the top of his head, she was very much afflicted with sorrow, cried like an osprey and
abused Kaikeyi who was the originator of the present calamity (as follows):

Seetha was shocked, fell unconscious at the sight of Rama's head and bow. She regained consciousness and asked Ravana to kill her also, so that she would join her husband in heaven. Meanwhile, Ravana is abruptly called away by an emissary and after consultation with his ministers, prepares his army for action against the forces of Rama.
