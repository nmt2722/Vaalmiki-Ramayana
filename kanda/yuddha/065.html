Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 65

Kumbhakarna rebukes Mahodara for rendering a wrong advice to Ravana

Kumbhakarna rebukes Mahodara for rendering a wrong advice to Ravana. He asserts Ravana, saying that he will annihilate Rama in battle.

57Yuddha Kanda 65.57

विपुलपरिघवान्स कुम्भकर्णो |
रिपुनिधनाय विनिःसृतो महात्मा |
कपि गणभयमाददत्सुभीमं |
प्रभुरिव किम्ण्करदण्डवान्युगान्ते

vipulaparighavānsa kumbhakarṇo |
ripunidhanāya viniḥsṛto mahātmā |
kapi gaṇabhayamādadatsubhīmaṃ |
prabhuriva kimṇkaradaṇḍavānyugānte

Wielding a large iron rod for the destroyable of the enemies, that gigantic
Kumbhakarna looked like Yama the lord of death armed with a rod of punishment, waiting upon him as
his attendant at the time of dissolution of the world and caused a great terrific fear to the
troops of monkeys.

waiting upon him like an attendant;yugaante= at the time of the dissolution of the world;aadadat= caused;subhiimam= a great terrific;kapigaNabhayam= fear to the troops of monkeys.

Kumbhakarna rebukes Mahodara for rendering a wrong advice to Ravana. He asserts Ravana, saying that he will annihilate Rama in battle. Ravana gets pleased and orders Kumbhakarna to wipe off the monkeys together with Rama and Lakshmana in battle. Then, Ravana arranged for ornamentation of the various limbs of Kumbhakarna with various types of jewellery, before sending him to the battle. As Kumbhakarna sallies forth to the battle, several bad omens appear on all sides. Kumbhakarna disregards these portents and marches ahead for the battle. Seeing the colossal form of Kumbhakarna, all the monkeys get frightened and take to their heels.
