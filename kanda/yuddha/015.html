Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 15

Indrajit criticizes Vibhishana for underestimating the former`s strength

Indrajit criticizes Vibhishana for underestimating the former`s strength. Vibhishana abuses Indrajit for his immaturity of mind and for his rash nature in underscoring the power of Rama`s arrows.

9Yuddha Kanda 15.9

13Yuddha Kanda 15.13

14Yuddha Kanda 15.14

धनानि रत्नानि सुभूषणानि |

" O, king! Offering riches, precious stones, good jewellery, excellent clothes,
bright colored gems and the god-like Sita to Rama, let us live here, free from anguish."

14. raajan= O;
king;nivedya= offering;dhanaani= riches;ratnaani= precious stones;subhuushhaNaani= good jewels;divyaani= excellent;vasaamsi= clothes;chitraan= bright colored;maNiimshcha= gems;siitaamdeviimcha= and the god-like Sita;raamaaya= to Rama;vasesu= let us live;iha= here;vita shokaH= free from anguish.

Indrajit criticizes Vibhishana for underestimating the former`s strength. Vibhishana abuses Indrajit for his immaturity of mind and for his rash nature in underscoring the power of Rama`s arrows. Vibhishana further advises Ravana to give back Sita along with valuable presents to Rama and to live happily ever after in Lanka.
