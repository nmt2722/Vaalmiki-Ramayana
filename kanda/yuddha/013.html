Yuddha Kanda · Sarga 13

Advised by Mahaparsva to take charge of Sita by force…

Advised by Mahaparsva to take charge of Sita by force, Ravana expresses his inability to do so, because of a curse pronounced against him by Brahma (lord of creation) in the past. Ravana then boasts of his own prowess.

1Yuddha Kanda 13.1

रावणम् क्रुद्धमाज्ञाय महापार्श्वो महाबलः |
मुहूर्त मनुसंचिन्त्य प्राञ्जलिर्वाक्य मब्रवीत्

rāvaṇam kruddhamājñāya mahāpārśvo mahābalaḥ |
muhūrta manusaṃcintya prāñjalirvākya mabravīt

Perceiving Ravana enraged, a very strong demon named Mahaparsva thought for a while
and with joined palms, spoke as follows:

2Yuddha Kanda 13.2

"That man indeed is a fool, who after reaching a forest inhabited by wild beasts and

3Yuddha Kanda 13.3

4Yuddha Kanda 13.4

बलात्कुक्कुटवृत्तेन प्रवर्तस्व महाबल |
अक्रम्याक्रम्य सीताम् वै ताम् भुङिक्स्व च रमस्व च

7Yuddha Kanda 13.7

उपप्रदानम् सान्त्वम् वा भेदम् वा कुशलैः कृतम् |
समतिक्रम्य दण्डेन सिद्धिमर्थेषु रोचये

10Yuddha Kanda 13.10

21Yuddha Kanda 13.21

न वासवेनापि सहस्रचक्षुषा |
युधास्मि शक्यो वरुणेन वा पुनः |

"Even Devendra (lord of celestials) with thousand eyes or Varuna (king of the

(Lord of riches);nirjitaa= was conquered;mayaa= by me;baahubalena= with the power of my arms."

Advised by Mahaparsva to take charge of Sita by force, Ravana expresses his inability to do so, because of a curse pronounced against him by Brahma (lord of creation) in the past. Ravana then boasts of his own prowess.
