Kanda I

बालकाण्ड

Bala Kanda

The Book of Childhood

The seed of the epic. Valmiki asks the sage Narada who the greatest man is, and is told the story of Rama. We follow Rama's birth in Ayodhya, his boyhood with his brothers, the breaking of Shiva's bow, his marriage to Sita, and the first journey with Vishvamitra into the forests of the rakshasas.
