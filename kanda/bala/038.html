Bala Kanda · Sarga 38

Sage Vishvamitra narrates the legend of Sagara…

Sage Vishvamitra narrates the legend of Sagara, the earliest King of Ayodhya and Rama's forefather, who is childless, but who begets sixty thousand sons of merit and one son of demerit.

1Bala Kanda 38.1

तां कथां कौशिको रामे निवेद्य मधुराक्षरम् |
पुन रेवापरं वाक्यं काकुत्स्थमिदमब्रवीत्

tām kathām kauśiko rāme nivedya madhura akṣaram |
punaḥ eva aparam vākyam kākutstham idam abravīt

Describing that legend of Kaartikeya to Rama, the descendent of Kaushi,
namely sage Vishvamitra has further spoken this best sentence worded
melodiously to Rama, the descendent of Kakutstha. [1-38-1]

1. kaushikaH= descendent of Kaushika, Vishvamitra;taam kathaam= that, legend of Kaarthikeya;raame nivedya= on submitting - describing to Rama;punaH eva= again, thus - further;idam aparam vaakyam= this, best, sentence;madhuraakSaram= in melodious words;kaakutstham abraviit= spoke to Rama of Kakutstha.

2Bala Kanda 38.2

अयोध्याधिपतिर्वीरः पूर्वमासीन्नराधिपः |
सगरो नाम धर्मात्मा प्रजाकामः स चाप्रजः

ayodhyā adhipatiḥ vīraḥ pūrvam āsīt narādhipaḥ |
sagaro nāma dharmātmā prajākāmaḥ sa ca aprajaḥ

"Once there was a valiant and virtue-souled king of Ayodhya named Sagara,
who is without offspring, as such he was longing for offspring. [1-38-2]

3Bala Kanda 38.3

वैदर्भदुहिता राम केशिनी नाम नामतः |
ज्येष्ठा सगरपत्नी सा धर्मिष्ठा सत्यवादिनी

vaidarbha duhitā rāma keśinī nāma nāmataḥ |
jyeṣṭhā sagara patnī sā dharmiṣṭhā satya vādinī

"The daughter of King of Vidarbha, renowned by name Keshini, is the

4Bala Kanda 38.4

अरिष्ठनेमिदुहिता सुपर्णभगिनी तु सा |

5Bala Kanda 38.5

ताभ्यां सह महाराजा पत्नीभ्याम् तप्तवांस्तपः |
हिमवंतं समासाद्य भृगुप्रस्रवणे गिरौ

tābhyām saha mahārājā patnībhyām taptavān tapaḥ |
himavaṃtam samāsādya bhṛgu prasravaṇe girau

"Reaching the Himalayan Mountains along with his two wives that

5. taabhyaam patniibhyaam saha= along with them two wives ;[saH= he that];mahaaraajaa= great-king;himavantam samaasaadya= on well-reaching Himalayas;bhR^iguprasravaNe girau= on Brhugu-prasavana, mountain;tapaH taptavaan= he undertook ascesis;

The Mt. Brugu-prsravana is said to be among Himalayan range on which

6Bala Kanda 38.6

अथ वर्षशते पूर्णे तपसाराधितो मुनिः |
सगराय वरं प्रादाद् भृगुः सत्यवतां वरः

atha varṣa śate pūrṇe tapasā ārādhito muniḥ |
sagarāya varam prādād bhṛguḥ satyavatām varaḥ

"On completion of a hundred years of ascesis, and when the Sage Bhrugu

7Bala Kanda 38.7

अपत्यलाभः सुमहान् भविष्यति तवानघ |
कीर्तिं चाप्रतिमां लोके प्राप्स्यसे पुरुषर्षभ

apatya lābhaḥ sumahān bhaviṣyati tava anagha |

8Bala Kanda 38.8

एका जनयिता तात पुत्रं वंशकरं तव |
षष्टिं पुत्रसहस्राणि ह्यपरा जनयिष्यति

ṣaṣṭim putra sahasrāṇi aparā janayiṣyati

" 'One of your wives will mother a son who enriches your dynasty, oh,

9Bala Kanda 38.9

भाषमाणं महात्मानं राजपुत्र्यौ प्रसाद्य तम् |
ऊचतुः परमप्रीते कृतांजलिपुटे तदा

bhāṣamāṇam mahātmānam rāja putryau prasādya tam |
ūcatuḥ parama prīte kṛtāṃjali puṭe tadā

Highly gladdened are the daughters of kings of Vidarbha and Aristanemi,

10Bala Kanda 38.10

एकः कस्याः सुतो ब्रह्मन् का बहून् जनयिष्यति |
श्रोतुमिच्छावहे ब्रह्मन् सत्यमस्तु वचस्तव

ekaḥ kasyāḥ suto brahman kā bahūn janayiṣyati |
śrotum icchāvahe brahman satyam astu vacaḥ tava

" 'Which of us will carry one son, oh, Brahma-sage, and which of us
begets numerous sons, we two wish to listen about that, oh, Brahman,
let your word betide truth.' Thus, the queens of Sagara spoke to the
sage. [1-38-10]

10. brahman= oh Brahma-sage;kasyaaH ekaH sutaH= to whom, one, son - will be there;kaa bahuun janayiSyati= who is she begets numerous [sons] ;brahman= oh Brahman;shrotum icChaavahe= we two wish to listen;tava vacaH satyam astu= let your word come true.

11Bala Kanda 38.11

तयोस्तद्वचनं श्रुत्वा भृगुः परमधार्मिकः |
उवाच परमां वाणीं स्वच्छन्दोऽत्र विधीयताम्

12Bala Kanda 38.12

एको वंशकरो वास्तु बहवो वा महाबलाः |
कीर्तिमन्तो महोत्साहाः का वा कं वरमिच्छति

eko vaṃśa karo vā astu bahavo vā mahābalāḥ |

'Whether one wants one son who enriches dynasty, or whether numerous

12. vamshakaraH ekaH astu vaa= dynasty enriching, one son, there shall be, whether;mahaabalaaH= highly energetic ones;kiirtimantaH= highly renowned ones;mahotsaahaaH= highly enthusiastic ones;bahavaH vaa= numerous, whether;kaa vaa= who, either - among you two;kam varam icChati= what, boon, wanted.

13Bala Kanda 38.13

16Bala Kanda 38.16

17Bala Kanda 38.17

18Bala Kanda 38.18

20Bala Kanda 38.20

स च ज्येष्ठो नरश्रेष्ठ सगरस्यात्मसंभवः |
बालान् गृहीत्वा तु जले सरय्वा रघुनंदन

21Bala Kanda 38.21

एवं पापसमाचारः सज्जनप्रतिबाधकः
पौराणामहिते युक्तः पित्रा निर्वासितः पुरात्

evam pāpa samācāraḥ sajjana pratibādhakaḥ
paurāṇām ahite yuktaḥ pitrā nirvāsitaḥ purāt

24Bala Kanda 38.24

स कृत्वा निश्चयं राजा सोपाध्यायगणस्तदा |
यज्ञकर्मणि वेदज्ञो यष्टुं समुपचक्रमे

24b, c. tadaa= then;veda~jnaH= knower of - expert in Veda-s ;saH raajaa= he, that king Sagara;sopaadhyaayagaNaH= along with assemblages of religious-teachers;yajnakarmaNi= in respect of Vedic-ritual's proceedings;nishcayam kR^itvaa= on making decision;yaSTum samupacakrame= commenced to perform ritual [well near started] .

Sage Vishvamitra narrates the legend of Sagara, the earliest King of Ayodhya and Rama's forefather, who is childless, but who begets sixty thousand sons of merit and one son of demerit. Emperor Sagara is the one who brought celestial Ganga onto the surface of earth..

Sage Vishvamirtra narrates the legend of Sagara, the earliest King of Ayodhya and Rama's forefather. His childlessness and begetting sixty thousand sons of merit and one son of demerit are narrated. Later King Sagara proceeds with the performance of a Vedic-ritual.
