Bala Kanda · Sarga 7

The truthful and upright characters of the ministers of Dasharatha…

The truthful and upright characters of the ministers of Dasharatha are portrayed, who make the rulership meaningful with their virtuous, skilful and efficient administration.

1Bala Kanda 7.1

तस्यामात्या गुणैरासन्निक्ष्वाकोस्तु महात्मनः |
मंत्रज्ञाश्चेङ्गितज्ञाश्च नित्यं प्रियहिते रताः

tasya amātyā guṇair āsan ikṣhvakostu mahātmanaḥ |
maṃtrajñāḥ ca iṅgitajñāḥ ca nityam priya hite ratāḥ

The misters for the great soul from Ikshvaku kings of Emperor Dasharatha, are
epitomised ones of their tactfulness, adroitness and are always obliged to undertake
welfare activities of their king and the kingdom. [1-7-1]

1. mahaaatmanaH tasya ikShwaakoH tu= for the great soul from Ikshvaku kings, Dasharatha;guNaiH= those with epitomised attribute;mantraj~naaH cha= tactful, also;iN^gitaj~naaH cha= adroit ones, also;nityam priyahite rataH= always, obliged to undertake welfare alone;amaatyaaH= ministers;aasan= are there.

4Bala Kanda 7.4

ऋत्विजौ द्वावभिमतौ तस्यास्तामृषिसत्तमौ |
वशिष्ठो वामदेवश्च मंत्रिणश्च तथापरे

ṛtvijau dvau abhimatau tasyāḥ tām ṛṣi sattamau |
vaśiṣṭho vāmadevaḥ ca maṃtriṇaḥ ca tathā apare

Two venerable saints of eminence are religious ministers for they are authorities

5Bala Kanda 7.5

सुयज्ञोऽप्यथ जाबालिः काश्यपोऽप्यथ गौतमः |
मार्कण्डेयस्तु दीर्घायुस्तथा कात्यायनो द्विजः
एतैर्ब्रह्मर्षिभिर्नित्यमृत्विजस्तस्य पौर्वकाः

suyajño api atha jābāliḥ kāśaypo api atha gautamaḥ |
mārkaṇḍeyaḥ tu dīrghāyuḥ tathā kātyāyano dvijaḥ
etaiḥ brahmarṣibhir nityam ṛtvijaḥ tasya paurvakāḥ

Suyajna, Jabaali, Kaashyapa, Gautama, Maarkandeya, Deerghaayu, and then Kaatyayana

6Bala Kanda 7.6

विद्याविनीता ह्रीमंतः कुशला नियतेन्द्रियाः

vidyā vinītā hrīmaṃtaḥ kuśalā niyatendriyāḥ

All the ministers are well versed in scriptures, they shun bad deeds, skilful
ones in their duties with their senses regulated. Those great souls are affluent,
knowers of all sciences, firmly courageous, and they are distinguished and quiet-souls,
and those ministers are true to their word. They are magnificent, patient and
famed ones and they smile afore they converse. [1-7-6b, 8a]

6b, 8a vidyaaviniitaaH= well-versed in scriptures;hriimantaH= shun bad deeds;kushalaaH= skilful;niyatendriyaaH= with regulated senses;shriimantaH cha= affluent ones;mahaaatmaanaH= great souls;shaastraj~naaH= knowers of sciences;dhR^iDhavikramaaH= firmly courageous;kiirtimantaH= distinguished ones;praNihitaaH= quiet souls;yathaavachanakaariNaH= doers as per their word, [true to their word];tejaHkShamaayashaHpraaptaaH= having magnificence, patience, fame;smitapuurvaabhibhaaShiNaH= smile afore while they converse.

8Bala Kanda 7.8

क्रोधात्कामार्थहेतोर्वा न ब्रूयुरनृतं वचः

krodhāt kāma artha hetor vā na brūyur anṛtam vacaḥ

They never speak untruthful words in anger or in greed or for monetary reasons
either. There is nothing unknown to them, even a little, in their own country
or in the others either, or about everything that is happening or has happened,
or that is going to happen, for they know them through agents. [1-7-8b, 9]

11Bala Kanda 7.11

24. mantrahite niviSTaiH= conducive to strategies, concerned;anuraktaiH= interested in;kushalaiH= well skilled;samarthaiH= efficient;taiH mantribhiH= with those, ministers;vR^itaH= surrounded by, in the company of;saH= he;paarthivaH= king;uditaH arkaH [yathaa]= rising, Sun [as with];yuktaH tejomayaiH gobhiH= having resplendent sunrays;diiptim avaapa= brilliance, obtained.

The truthful and upright characters of the ministers of Dasharatha are portrayed, who make the rulership meaningful with their virtuous, skilful and efficient administration. Along with the political ministers, the religious ministry is also portrayed.
