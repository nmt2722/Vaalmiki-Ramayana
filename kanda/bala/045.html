1Bala Kanda 45.1

विश्वामित्रवचः श्रुत्वा राघवः सहलक्ष्मणः |
विस्मयं परमं गत्वा विश्वामित्रमथाब्रवीत्

viśvāmitra vacaḥ śrutvā rāghavaḥ saha lakṣmaṇaḥ |
vismayam paramam gatvā viśvāmitram atha abravīt

On listening the narration of Sage Vishwamitra about Ganga's descent along with
Lakshmana, Rama went into a great astonishment, and then addressed Vishvamitra.
[1-45-1]

2Bala Kanda 45.2

अत्यद्भुतमिदं ब्रह्मन् कथितं परमं त्वया |
गंगावतरणं पुण्यं सागरस्यापि पूरणम्

ati adbhutam idam brahman kathitam paramam tvayā |
gaṃgā avataraṇam puṇyam sāgarasya api pūraṇam

3Bala Kanda 45.3

क्षणभूतेव नौ रात्रिः संवृत्तेयं परंतप |
इमां चिंतयतोः सर्वां निखिलेन कथां तव

kṣaṇa bhūta iva nau rātriḥ saṃvṛtta iyam paraṃtapa |

4Bala Kanda 45.4

[1-45-4]

Here the placement of verses does not give a continuity of narration in this version,

5Bala Kanda 45.5

6Bala Kanda 45.6

गता भगवती रात्रिः श्रोतव्यं परमं श्रुतम् |
तराम सरितं श्रेष्ठं पुण्यां त्रिपथगां नदीम्

7Bala Kanda 45.7

नौरेषा हि सुखास्तीर्णा ऋषीणां पुण्यकर्मणाम् |
भगवन्तमिह प्राप्तं ज्ञात्वा त्वरितमागता

nauḥ eṣā hi sukha āstīrṇā ṛṣīṇām puṇya karmaṇām |
bhagavaṃtam iha prāptam jñātvā tvaritam āgatā

"This boat which is suitable for the embarkation of the sages whose deeds are pious,

8Bala Kanda 45.8

9Bala Kanda 45.9

uttaram tīram āsādya saṃpūjya ṛṣi gaṇam tataḥ |

10Bala Kanda 45.10

11Bala Kanda 45.11

12Bala Kanda 45.12

कतमो राजवंशोऽयं विशालायां महामुने |

14Bala Kanda 45.14

श्रूयतां राम शक्रस्य कथां कथयतः श्रुताम् |

15Bala Kanda 45.15

पूर्वं कृतयुगे राम दितेः पुत्रा महाबलाः |
अदितेश्च महाभागा वीर्यवन्तः सुधार्मिकाः

pūrvam kṛta yuge rāma diteḥ putrā mahābalāḥ |
aditeḥ ca mahābhāgā vīryavantaḥ sudhārmikāḥ

Once in Krita era, oh, Rama, the sons of Lady Diti were extremely energetic, whereas
the sons of her younger sister Lady Aditi were vigorous and highly righteous. [1-45-15]

15. mahaabhaagaa raama= oh, highly fortunate, Rama;puurvam kR^itayuge= once, in Krita era;diteH putraaH= Diti's, sons;mahaabalaaH= extremely energetic ones;aditeH ca= Aditi's, also ;viiryavantaH= vigorous ones;sudhaarmikaaH= highly righteous ones.

Diti is the elder sister of Aditi and the first wife of Sage Kaashyapa. Her sons
were originally calledasura-s, and later given the nomenclature
ofraakshasaa-s, the demons. Lady Aditi the second wife of that sage Kaashyapa,
gave birth to the progeny ofsura-s, later generally called asdevataa-s, the gods. These two ladies are the daughters of Daksha Prajaapati.

17Bala Kanda 45.17

18Bala Kanda 45.18

19Bala Kanda 45.19

अथ वर्षसहस्रेण योक्त्रं सर्पशिरांसि च |
वमन्त्यतिविषं तत्र ददंशुर्दशनैः शिलाः

20Bala Kanda 45.20

22Bala Kanda 45.22

23Bala Kanda 45.23

उवाचैनं स्मितं कृत्वा रुद्रं शूलधरं हरिः |

25Bala Kanda 45.25

Legend/puraaNa: Once, when Indra was riding his elephant Iravata, Sage
Duurvasa came towards him and offered a garland, respecting Indra as the administrator of the universe.
Indra decorated that garland on the head of his elephant and that elephant removed it and trampled.

and all his luxurious paraphernalia be submerged in ocean. Accordingly, Indra looses everything and
becomes destitute, and he then approaches Vishnu. Vishnu reprimands Indra for ridiculing elderly sages
like Duurvasa and then plans to churn the ocean to retrieve all the lost treasures. Thus, the legend
and the churning are going on for ages.

From thekundalini / power of yogapoint of view the churning of ocean
is continually going on in everyone's body. The body is the ocean and at its abyss, the triangle in
the perineal or sacral plexus is the base of Mt. Mandara. The churning rod is the spinal cord in the
rod like backbone and the churning strings are theida, pingalanamely,
the sympathetic and parasympathetic systems. The churners are the air elements of vital forces on either
side. This total system is held upright by Vishnu because He isnaaraayaNa, nara
+ aayaNa'one who conducts humans, say created beings, into being and lead them away, again into
His own self...' Whenkundalini poweris incited the first results are
always negative like,haalaaha,'the lethal poison...' and all all-ruinous
ills befall on us. If this negativity is surrendered to someone who can handle it effortlessly, like
Shiva, then the positivity results likeAmrita/Ambrosia.Even if this elixir
of life is readily available, there will be still better lures, lusts, and longings like Mohini and
if enchanted by those instantaneous quirks, which are yet another kind of negativity, we will be lost
completely, like the demons. Hence 'churn the correct way...' is the postulate of yoga.

The legend of churning Milk Ocean is narrated to Rama and Lakshmana when they reach and see a city named Vishaala. Vishvamitra narrates howhaalaahala, the lethal poison as well asAmrita, the ambrosial elixir emerged from the churning of Milk Ocean, and how Shiva contained the poison and how Vishnu helped the churning in His incarnation as Tortoise.
