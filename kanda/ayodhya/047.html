The multitude that had caused Rama in his journey to the forest wake…

The multitude that had caused Rama in his journey to the forest wake up to find Rama and his associates gone.

1Ayodhya Kanda 47.1

प्रभातायाम् तु शर्वर्याम् पौराः ते राघवो विना |
शोक उपहत निश्चेष्टा बभूवुर् हत चेतसः

prabhātāyām tu śarvaryām paurāḥ te rāghavo vinā |
śoka upahata niśceṣṭā babhūvur hata cetasaḥ

As the night was beginning to become dawn, those citizens relinquished by Raghava
were perplexed with grief and became motionless.

1. sharvaryaam= As the night;prabhataayaam= was beginning to become day light;te pouraaH= those citizens;raaghavam vinaa= relinquished by Rama;babhuuvaH= became;hatachetasaH= perplexed;shokopahatachetasaH= their hearts stricken by grief;nishcheshhTaaH= and motionless.

2Ayodhya Kanda 47.2

शोकज अश्रु परिद्यूना वीक्षमाणाः ततः ततः |
आलोकम् अपि रामस्य न पश्यन्ति स्म दुह्खिताः

śokaja aśru paridyūnā vīkṣamāṇāḥ tataḥ tataḥ |
ālokam api rāmasya na paśyanti sma duhkhitāḥ

Made miserable with tears born of anguish and agony, they could not notice even a
glimmer of Rama, though casting their eyes everywhere.

2. shokajaashru paridyuunaaH= Made miserable with tears born of anguish;duHkhitaaH= and agony;viikshhamaaNaaH= seeing;tatastataH= in that and that place;na pashyantisma= they could not notice;aalokamapi= even a glimmer;raamasya= of Rama.

3Ayodhya Kanda 47.3

ते विषादार्तवदना रहितास्तेन धिमता |
कृपणाः करुणा वाचो वदन्ति स्म मनस्विनः

te viṣādārtavadanā rahitāstena dhimatā |
kṛpaṇāḥ karuṇā vāco vadanti sma manasvinaḥ

Their faces distorted with sorrow, deprived as they were of sagacious Rama and
therefore non-pulsed, the citizens broke into piteous exclamations, saying:-

4Ayodhya Kanda 47.4

धिगस्तु खलु निद्राम् ताम् ययापहृतचेतसः |
नाद्य पश्यामहे रामम् पृथूरस्कम् महाभुजम्

dhigastu khalu nidrām tām yayāpahṛtacetasaḥ |
nādya paśyāmahe rāmam pṛthūraskam mahābhujam

"Cursed be to that slumber, rendered unconscious, by which we could not behold today

5Ayodhya Kanda 47.5

कथम् नाम महाबाहुः स तथाऽवितथक्रियः |
भक्तम् जनम् परित्यज्य प्रवासम् राघवो गतः

6Ayodhya Kanda 47.6

यो नः सदा पालयति पिता पुत्रानिवौरसान् |
कथम् रघूणाम् स श्रेष्ठस्त्यक्त्वा नो विपिनम् गतः

yo naḥ sadā pālayati pitā putrānivaurasān |
katham raghūṇām sa śreṣṭhastyaktvā no vipinam gataḥ

"How did the chief of Raghus, who protected us ever, like a father the children born

7Ayodhya Kanda 47.7

इहैव निधनम् यामो महाप्रस्थानमेव वा |
रामेण रहितानाम् हि किमर्थम् जीवितम् हि नः

ihaiva nidhanam yāmo mahāprasthānameva vā |
rāmeṇa rahitānām hi kimartham jīvitam hi naḥ

"Let us have recourse to death here itself, or definitely set out for a grand

as we are from Rama?"

8Ayodhya Kanda 47.8

सन्ति शुष्काणि काष्ठानि प्रभूतानि महान्ति च |
तैः प्रज्वाल्य चिताम् सर्वे प्रविशामोऽथ पावकम्

santi śuṣkāṇi kāṣṭhāni prabhūtāni mahānti ca |
taiḥ prajvālya citām sarve praviśāmo.atha pāvakam

9Ayodhya Kanda 47.9

किम् व्ख्स्यामो महाबाहुरनसूयः प्रियम्वद |
नीतः स राघवोऽस्माभिर्ति वक्तुम् कथम् क्षमम्

10Ayodhya Kanda 47.10

सा नूनम् नगरी दीना दृष्ट्वाऽस्मान् राघवम् विना |
भविष्यति निरानन्दा सस्त्रीबालवयोधिका

sā nūnam nagarī dīnā dṛṣṭvā.asmān rāghavam vinā |

11Ayodhya Kanda 47.11

निर्यातास्तेन वीरेण सह नित्यम् जितात्मना |
विहिनास्तेन च पुनः कथम् पश्याम ताम् पुरीम्

niryātāstena vīreṇa saha nityam jitātmanā |
vihināstena ca punaḥ katham paśyāma tām purīm

"Having left the city thus with that hero, the conqueror of one's own self, how can

12Ayodhya Kanda 47.12

इतीव बहुधा वाचो बाहुमुद्यम्य ते जनाः |
विलपन्तिस्म दुःखर्ता विवत्सा इव धेनवः

itīva bahudhā vāco bāhumudyamya te janāḥ |
vilapantisma duḥkhartā vivatsā iva dhenavaḥ

Uplifting their arms , those men stricken with anguish, like cows bereft of their
calves, lamented in various ways as above.

13Ayodhya Kanda 47.13

ततः मार्ग अनुसारेण गत्वा किंचित् क्षणम् पुनः
मार्ग नाशात् विषादेन महता समभिप्लुतः

tataḥ mārga anusāreṇa gatvā kiṃcit kṣaṇam punaḥ
mārga nāśāt viṣādena mahatā samabhiplutaḥ

Then, proceeding to some distance along the tracks for a while, they were
overwhelmed with great despair again due to sudden disappearance of tracks.

14Ayodhya Kanda 47.14

रथस्य मार्ग नाशेन न्यवर्तन्त मनस्विनः |
किम् इदम् किम् करिष्यामः दैवेन उपहताइति

rathasya mārga nāśena nyavartanta manasvinaḥ |
kim idam kim kariṣyāmaḥ daivena upahatāiti

The good-natured citizens, due to disappearance of the tracks of the chariot,
returned to Ayodhya, saying thus, " How is it? What shall we do? We are afflicted by
providence"

15Ayodhya Kanda 47.15

ततः यथा आगतेन एव मार्गेण क्लान्त चेतसः |

16Ayodhya Kanda 47.16

17Ayodhya Kanda 47.17

एषा रामेण नगरी रहिता नातिशोभते |

18Ayodhya Kanda 47.18

चन्द्रहीनमिवाकाशम् तोयहीनमिवार्णवम् |
अपश्यन्निहतानन्दम् नगरम् ते विचेतसः

candrahīnamivākāśam toyahīnamivārṇavam |

19Ayodhya Kanda 47.19

ते तानि वेश्मानि महाधनानि |
दुःखेन दुःखोपहता विशन्तः |
नैव प्रजज्ञुः स्वजनम् जनम् वा |
निरीक्षमाणाः प्रविणष्टहर्षाः

te tāni veśmāni mahādhanāni |
duḥkhena duḥkhopahatā viśantaḥ |
naiva prajajñuḥ svajanam janam vā |
nirīkṣamāṇāḥ praviṇaṣṭaharṣāḥ

Entering their houses full of abundant riches with uneasiness, the citizens could
not distinguish between their own people and others, even though being looked at, stricken as they
were with grief, their joy having altogether extinguished.

19. te= they;praviNashhTa harshhaaH= having lost their joy;vishantaH= entering;taani= those;veshmaani= houses;mahaadhanaani= with abundant riches ;duHkhena= uneasily;duHkhopahataaH= having stricken with grief;naiva prajaG^yuH= could not dishtinguish;svajanam= their own people;janam vaa= or others;niriikshhamaanaaH= (even if) being looked at.

The multitude that had caused Rama in his journey to the forest wake up to find Rama and his associates gone. They begin to rebuke themselves. Overwhelmed with grief, they hunt up to tracks of Rama's chariot. Being unable to find the tracks, they helplessly return to Ayodhya in sheer despair.
