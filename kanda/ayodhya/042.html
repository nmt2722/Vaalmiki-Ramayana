Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 42

The dust raised by Rama's chariot having disappeared…

The dust raised by Rama's chariot having disappeared for a while, Dasaratha feels dejected and fell insensible on ground.

1Ayodhya Kanda 42.1

यावत् तु निर्यतः तस्य रजो रूपम् अदृश्यत |
न एव इक्ष्वाकु वरः तावत् सम्जहार आत्म चक्षुषी

yāvat tu niryataḥ tasya rajo rūpam adṛśyata |
na eva ikṣvāku varaḥ tāvat samjahāra ātma cakṣuṣī

As long as the dust raised by the wheels of Rama's chariot could be seen, so long
did the king not withdraw his gaze from it.

2Ayodhya Kanda 42.2

yāvad rājā priyam putram paśyati atyanta dhārmikam |
tāvad vyavardhata iva asya dharaṇyām putra darśane

3Ayodhya Kanda 42.3

30Ayodhya Kanda 42.30

34Ayodhya Kanda 42.34

तम् रामम् एव अनुविचिन्तयन्तम् |
समीक्ष्य देवी शयने नर इन्द्रम् |

upa upaviśya adhikam ārta rūpāupa |
vinihśvasantī vilalāpa kṛccram

Perceiving that monarch on the coach absorbed in the thought of Rama alone, Kausalya
appeared very sad and sitting beside him, began to sigh and lament with a distressed look.

34. samiikshhya= perceiving;tam narendram= that monarch;shayane= on the coach ;anuvichintayantam = absorbed in the thought ;raamam eva = of Rama alone;devii= Kausalya;adhikam aartaruupaa= appeared very sad ;upaavishya = and sitting down beside him ;viniHshvasantii = began to sigh;vilalaapa= and lament;kR^ichchham= with distressed look.

The dust raised by Rama's chariot having disappeared for a while, Dasaratha feels dejected and fell insensible on ground. When Kaikeyi comes forward to support him he scolds and asks her not to touch him. Kausalya then lifts him up and persuades him to return to their house. His personal attendants take him to Kausalya's apartments and seeing him plunged in grief, Kausalya seats herself beside him and began to lament in various ways.
