Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 32

Rama bestows parting gifts to Vedic scholars and their wives

Rama bestows parting gifts to Vedic scholars and their wives. Thereafter he also accords charities to Brahmins, young scholars, servants, and others.

whom was Yagnavalkya, Subsequently Vaisampayana being offended with Yaganavalkya, made him disgorge
