Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 62

After hearing harsh words from Kausalya, king Dasaratha loses consciousness…

After hearing harsh words from Kausalya, king Dasaratha loses consciousness and restores it after a long time.

1Ayodhya Kanda 62.1

एवम् तु क्रुद्धया राजा राम मात्रा सशोकया |
श्रावितः परुषम् वाक्यम् चिन्तयाम् आस दुह्खितः

evam tu kruddhayā rājā rāma mātrā saśokayā |
śrāvitaḥ paruṣam vākyam cintayām āsa duhkhitaḥ

When harsh words with anger in this manner by the grief-stricken Kausalya, Rama's
mother were heard, king Dasaratha felt depressed and reflected upon them.

2Ayodhya Kanda 62.2

cintayitvā sa ca nṛpo mumoha vyākulendriyaḥ |
atha dīrgheṇa kālena samjñāmāpa paratapaḥ

10Ayodhya Kanda 62.10

21Ayodhya Kanda 62.21

तथ प्रह्लादितः वाक्यैः देव्या कौसल्यया नृपः |
शोकेन च समाक्रान्तः निद्राया वशम् एयिवान्

tatha prahlāditaḥ vākyaiḥ devyā kausalyayā nṛpaḥ |
śokena ca samākrāntaḥ nidrāyā vaśam eyivān

The king, thus cheered up by the queen Kausalya, got subjected to slumber, after
having been overcome by grief.

21. nR^ipaH= the king;tathaa= thus;prasaaditaH= cheered up;devyaa= by the queen;kausalyayaa= Kausalya;eyivaan= got;vasham= subjected;nidraayaaH= to sleep;samaakraantaH ca= after having been overcome;shokena= be grief.

After hearing harsh words from Kausalya, king Dasaratha loses consciousness and restores it after a long time. Afterwards, Kausalya repents for her mistake of speaking such crude words and consoles the king with her reconciliatory words. Overcome by grief, Dasaratha fell into the grip, Dasaratha fell into the grip of slumber as the night prevailed.
