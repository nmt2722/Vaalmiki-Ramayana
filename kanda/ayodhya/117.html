9Ayodhya Kanda 117.9

दश वर्षाण्य् अनावृष्ट्या दग्धे लोके निरन्तरम्

daśa varṣāṇy anāvṛṣṭyā dagdhe loke nirantaram

"O, Irreproachable Rama! When the earth was burnt up by drought without break for

of a divine command, in a great hurry ten nights were reduced to one night.* This Anasuya, who has
bathed after completion of the voes, is like a mother to you."

*We read in Puranas how sage Mandavya once pronounced a curse against a

hermittess, husband and accomplished the purpose of gods.

"Thus devoted to your lord, loyal to your husband, following established rules, you

Rama, after due reflection, decides to leave Chitrakuta mountain because of many handicaps. Setting out his journey from Chitrakuta mountain, he reaches the hermitage of Sage Atri and offers his salutation to him. Introducing his wife Anansuya as a great female ascetic to Rama, Atri urges Rama to send his concert Seetha to Anasuya. Anasuya receives Seetha, who greeted her and gives her instructions on the role and responsibilities of a devoted wife.
