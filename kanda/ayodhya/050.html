Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 50

Rama bids farewell to his birth-place Ayodhya…

Rama bids farewell to his birth-place Ayodhya and reaches the bank of the holy Ganga. There, Nishadas offers welcome reception to Rama and others.

विशालान् कोसलान् रम्यान् यात्वा लक्ष्मण पूर्वजः |
अयोध्याभिमुखो धीमान् प्राञ्ञ्लिर्वाक्वमब्रवीत्

viśālān kosalān ramyān yātvā lakṣmaṇa pūrvajaḥ |

face turned towards Ayodhya, the wise Rama with joined palms spoke the following words:

8Ayodhya Kanda 50.8
