अयोध्याकाण्ड

Ayodhya Kanda

The Book of Ayodhya

The heartbreak of exile. Dasharatha resolves to crown Rama, but Kaikeyi's old boons send the prince into the forest for fourteen years. Ayodhya grieves; Sita and Lakshmana follow him. The brothers cross the Ganga, meet Guha and Bharadvaja, and begin a life of renunciation, while Bharata refuses the throne.
