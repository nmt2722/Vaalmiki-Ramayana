Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 2

Dasaratha mentions his desire about coronation of Rama before the assembly…

Dasaratha mentions his desire about coronation of Rama before the assembly of people. All people in the assembly unanimously and delightfully accept it while describing all of Rama's virtues.

fulfiller of desires;
Dasaratha!;naH hitaaya= for our benefit;tvam= you;arhasi= are fit;kshhipram= immediately;mudaa= gladly;abhishhiktum= to inaugurate;tam= that;te aatmajaM= your son Rama;devadevopamaM= equal to the god of gods;
Vishnu;nivishhTam= who is interested;hite= in the benefit of;sarvasya= the entire;lokasya= world;udaarajushhTaM= who is possessing the best of virtues.

Dasaratha mentions his desire about coronation of Rama before the assembly of people. All people in the assembly unanimously and delightfully accept it while describing all of Rama's virtues.
