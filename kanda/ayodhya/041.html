Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 41

The ladies of the royal gymnasium lament over the exile of Rama and others

The ladies of the royal gymnasium lament over the exile of Rama and others. The citizens of Ayodhya also are reduced to a miserable plight. All Nature mourns at Rama's departure.

1Ayodhya Kanda 41.1

तस्मिंस्तु पुरुषव्याघ्रे विनिर्याति कृताञ्जलौ |
आर्तशब्दोऽथ सम्जज्ञे स्त्रीणामन्तह्पुते तदा

tasmiṃstu puruṣavyāghre viniryāti kṛtāñjalau |
ārtaśabdo.atha samjajñe strīṇāmantahpute tadā

great cry of distress arose among women of inner apartments.

6Ayodhya Kanda 41.6

7Ayodhya Kanda 41.7

8Ayodhya Kanda 41.8

9Ayodhya Kanda 41.9

न अग्नि होत्राणि अहूयन्त सूर्यः च अन्तर् अधीयत |
व्यसृजन् कवलान् नागा गावो वत्सान् न पाययन्

na agni hotrāṇi ahūyanta sūryaḥ ca antar adhīyata |
vyasṛjan kavalān nāgā gāvo vatsān na pāyayan

On the departure of Rama to the forest, the sacred fires were no longer performed,

10Ayodhya Kanda 41.10

व्यसृजन् कबलान्नागा गावो वत्सान्न पाययन् |

11Ayodhya Kanda 41.11

14Ayodhya Kanda 41.14

15Ayodhya Kanda 41.15

16Ayodhya Kanda 41.16

20Ayodhya Kanda 41.20

21Ayodhya Kanda 41.21

ततः तु अयोध्या रहिता महात्मना |
पुरंदरेण इव मही सपर्वता |
चचाल घोरम् भय भार पीडिता |
सनाग योध अश्व गणा ननाद च

tataḥ tu ayodhyā rahitā mahātmanā |
puraṃdareṇa iva mahī saparvatā |

Afflicted by fear and grief, the earth with its mountains, deprived of that great

a great uproar reigned in the city which was filled with elephants, warriors and horses.
