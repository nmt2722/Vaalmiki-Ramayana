Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 40

The departure of Rama from Ayodhya for his exile is depicted

The departure of Rama from Ayodhya for his exile is depicted. When they are charioted through the streets of Ayodhya the citizenry wails and weeps for Rama's disposition.
