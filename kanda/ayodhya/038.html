Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 38

Dasharatha is enraged to see his precious daughter-in-law in rags…

Dasharatha is enraged to see his precious daughter-in-law in rags and takes Kaikeyi to task. Rama seeks blessings of his father for departure and requests him to keep his mother Kausalya, who is aging, comfortable.

1Ayodhya Kanda 38.1

तस्याम् चीरम् वसानायाम् नाथवत्याम् अनाथवत् |
प्रचुक्रोश जनः सर्वो धिग् त्वाम् दशरथम् तु इति

"Oh, the king akim to the mighty Indra the ruler of gods! You must see that my
