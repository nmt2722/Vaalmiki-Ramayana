Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 8

Manthara with her wicked thoughts tries to convince Kaikeyi of the possible…

Manthara with her wicked thoughts tries to convince Kaikeyi of the possible misfortune that could occur to Kaikeyi and her family if Sri Rama becomes the King.

1Ayodhya Kanda 8.1

मन्थरा त्वभ्यसूयैनामुत्सृज्याभरणं च तत्|
उवाचेदं ततो वाक्यं कोपदुःखसमन्विता

2Ayodhya Kanda 8.2

हर्षं किमिदमस्थाने कृतवत्यसि बालिशे |
शोकसागरमध्यस्थमात्मानं नावबुध्यसे

śokasāgaramadhyasthamātmānaṃ nāvabudhyase

"O, foolish one! Not knowing that you are in the midst of an ocean of trouble, what

3Ayodhya Kanda 8.3

मनसा प्रहसामि त्वां देवि दुःखार्धिता सती |
यच्छोचितव्ये हृष्टासि प्राप्येदं व्यसनं महत्

manasā prahasāmi tvāṃ devi duḥkhārdhitā satī |
yacchocitavye hṛṣṭāsi prāpyedaṃ vyasanaṃ mahat

"Oh, queen! Though stricken with grief, I mentally laugh at you in that you are

4Ayodhya Kanda 8.4

शोचामि दुर्मतित्वं ते का हि प्राज्ञा प्रहर्षयेत् |
अरेः सपत्नीपुत्रस्य वृद्धिं मृत्युमिवागताम्

śocāmi durmatitvaṃ te kā hi prājñā praharṣayet |
areḥ sapatnīputrasya vṛddhiṃ mṛtyumivāgatām

"I am lamenting over your foolish mind. Does any intelligent woman feel happy over

5Ayodhya Kanda 8.5

भरतादेव रामस्य राज्यसाधारणाद्भयम् |
तद्विचिन्त्य विषण्णास्मि भय भीताद्धि जायते

bharatādeva rāmasya rājyasādhāraṇādbhayam |
tadvicintya viṣaṇṇāsmi bhaya bhītāddhi jāyate

"Rama has a fear about Bharata because Bharata has equal rights over the kingdom. In

6Ayodhya Kanda 8.6

लक्ष्मणो हि महेष्वासो रामं सर्वात्मना गतः |
शत्रुघ्नश्चापि भरतं काकुत्थ्सं लक्ष्मणो यथा

lakṣmaṇo hi maheṣvāso rāmaṃ sarvātmanā gataḥ |
śatrughnaścāpi bharataṃ kākutthsaṃ lakṣmaṇo yathā

"Lakshmana, wielding a great bow, joined Rama with all his heart. Shatrughna is as

7Ayodhya Kanda 8.7

प्रत्यासन्नक्रमेणापि भरतस्तैव भामिनि |
राज्यक्रमो विप्रकृष्टस्तयोस्तावत्कनीयसोः

pratyāsannakrameṇāpi bharatastaiva bhāmini |
rājyakramo viprakṛṣṭastayostāvatkanīyasoḥ

"Oh Kaikeyi! In line with even the proximity of birth; the claim to the throne of

8Ayodhya Kanda 8.8

विदुषः क्षत्रचारित्रे प्राज्ञस्य प्राप्तकारिणः |
भयात्प्रवेपे रामस्य चिन्तयन्ती तवात्मजम्

viduṣaḥ kṣatracāritre prājñasya prāptakāriṇaḥ |
bhayātpravepe rāmasya cintayantī tavātmajam

"Rama is a learned man and a political statesman. His actions are timely and

9Ayodhya Kanda 8.9

सुभगा खलु कौसल्या यस्याः पुत्रोऽभिषेक्ष्यते |
यौवराज्येन महता श्वः पुष्येण द्विजोत्तमैः

yauvarājyena mahatā śvaḥ puṣyeṇa dvijottamaiḥ

10Ayodhya Kanda 8.10

प्राप्तां सुमहतीं प्रीतिं प्रतीतां तां हतद्विषम् |
उपस्थास्यसि कौसल्यां दासीवत्त्वं कृताञ्जलिः

prāptāṃ sumahatīṃ prītiṃ pratītāṃ tāṃ hatadviṣam |

"With folded arms, as a maid-servant, you have to serve that Kausalya who having

11Ayodhya Kanda 8.11

एवम् चेत्त्वं सहास्माभिस्तस्याः प्रेष्य भविष्यसि |

12Ayodhya Kanda 8.12

हृष्टाः खलु भविष्यन्ति रामस्य परमाः स्त्रियः |
अप्रहृष्टा भविष्यन्ति स्नुषास्ते भरतक्षये

hṛṣṭāḥ khalu bhaviṣyanti rāmasya paramāḥ striyaḥ |
aprahṛṣṭā bhaviṣyanti snuṣāste bharatakṣaye

"Rama's wives will get delighted. Your daughters-in-law will be unhappy because of
Bharata's waning position."

12. raamasya= Rama's;paramaastriyaH= great wives;bhavantikhalu= will become;hR^ishTaaH= delighted;te= your;snushhaaH= daughters-in-law;bhavishhyanti= will become;aprahR^isTaaH= unhappy;bharata kshhaye= of Bharata's weak position.

Comment: The words 'Rama's wives' here do not indicate that Rama had multiple
wives. Manathara refers to a possible future where Rama being a King would marry other women. It
was a norm then for a king to have more than one wife.

14Ayodhya Kanda 8.14

धर्मज्ञो गुरुभिर्दान्तः कृतज्ञ सत्यवाक्चुचि |

15Ayodhya Kanda 8.15

16Ayodhya Kanda 8.16

17Ayodhya Kanda 8.17

सा त्वमभ्युदये प्राप्ते वर्तमाने च मन्थरे |
भविष्यति च कल्याणे किमर्थं परितप्यसे

"Oh, Manthara! When we got an occasion for rejoicing as at present and when a

jealousy)?"

21Ayodhya Kanda 8.21

26Ayodhya Kanda 8.26

सपत्निवृद्दौ या मे त्वं प्रदेयं दातुमिच्चिसि

"I came here in your interests. But you have not understood me. You think it fit to

27Ayodhya Kanda 8.27

ध्रुवं तु भरतं रामः प्राप्य राज्यमकण्टकम् |
देशान्तरं वासयिता लोकान्तरमथापि व

"Rama, ascending the throne without hindrance, will either send away Bharata to some

28Ayodhya Kanda 8.28

बाल एव हि मातुल्यं भरतो नायितस्त्वया |
सन्निकर्षाच्च सौहार्दं जायते स्थावरेष्वपि

bāla eva hi mātulyaṃ bharato nāyitastvayā |
sannikarṣācca sauhārdaṃ jāyate sthāvareṣvapi

"You sent Bharata to his maternal uncle's house even in his childhood. By being

29Ayodhya Kanda 8.29

भरतस्यानुवशगः शत्रुघ्नोऽपि समं गतः |
लक्ष्मणो हि यथा रामं तथासौ भरतं गतः

bharatasyānuvaśagaḥ śatrughno.api samaṃ gataḥ |

"Shatrughna was drawn towards Bharata and went along with him. Just as Lakshmana

Comment: Sage Valmiki might be implying here that if Shatrughna were to be
present then at Ayodhya, he would have tried for the prosperity of Bharata.

30Ayodhya Kanda 8.30

श्रूयते हि द्रुमः कश्चिच्चेत्तव्यो वनजीविभिः |
सन्निकर्षादिषीकाभिर्मो चितः परमाद्भयात्

śrūyate hi drumaḥ kaściccettavyo vanajīvibhiḥ |
sannikarṣādiṣīkābhirmo citaḥ paramādbhayāt

"We hear that a tree marked down for felling by foresters, when covered by thorny

31Ayodhya Kanda 8.31

गोप्ता हि रामं सौमित्रिर्लक्ष्मणं चापि राघवः |
अश्विनोरिव सौभ्रात्रं तयोर्लोकेषु विश्रुतम्

goptā hi rāmaṃ saumitrirlakṣmaṇaṃ cāpi rāghavaḥ |
aśvinoriva saubhrātraṃ tayorlokeṣu viśrutam

"Lakshmana protects Rama. Rama protects Lakshmana. Their brotherly love is as famous

32Ayodhya Kanda 8.32

तस्मान्न लक्ष्मणे रामः पापं किञ्चित्करिष्यति |
रामस्तु भरते पापं कुर्यादिति न संशयः

tasmānna lakṣmaṇe rāmaḥ pāpaṃ kiñcitkariṣyati |
rāmastu bharate pāpaṃ kuryāditi na saṃśayaḥ

"Hence, Rama will not do a sinful act of killing Lakshmana. However there is no

33Ayodhya Kanda 8.33

तस्माद्राजगृहादेव वनं गच्छतु ते सुतः |
एतद्धि रोचते मह्यं भृश्ं चापि हितं तव

34Ayodhya Kanda 8.34

एवं ते ज्ञातिपक्षस्य श्रेयश्चैव भविष्यति |
यदि चेद्भरतो धर्मात्पित्र्यं राज्यमवाप्स्यति

35Ayodhya Kanda 8.35

स ते सुखोचितो बालो रामस्य सहजो रिपुः |

36Ayodhya Kanda 8.36

37Ayodhya Kanda 8.37

38Ayodhya Kanda 8.38

39Ayodhya Kanda 8.39

यदा हि रामः पृथिवीमवाप्स्यति |
ध्रुवं प्रणष्टो भरतो भविष्यति |

"When Rama gets power of the kingdom Bharata will certainly get ruined. Hence, think

39. yadaahi= when;raamaH= Rama;avaapsyasi= gets;pr^ithiviim= the earth;bharataH= Bharata;bhavishhyati= will become;dhruvam= certainly;praNashhTaH= ruined;ataH= hence;saMchintaya= think of;raajyam= kingdom;aatmaje= to your son;vivaasakaaraNam= and causing exile;parasya= to your enemy;
Rama.

Manthara with her wicked thoughts tries to convince Kaikeyi of the possible misfortune that could occur to Kaikeyi and her family if Sri Rama becomes the King. Although Kaikeyi's initial reaction is to praise Rama's qualities; her mind is slowly but surely poisoned by Manathara's sinful words.
