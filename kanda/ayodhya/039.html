Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 39

Sumantra arranges for a chariot for the travel of Rama, Seetha…

Sumantra arranges for a chariot for the travel of Rama, Seetha, and Lakshmana at the orders of Dasharatha. Kingly ornaments are obtained for Seetha. Rama seeks blessings from his mother Kausalya and others too. Kausalya's parting advises to Seetha.

1Ayodhya Kanda 39.1

रामस्य तु वचः श्रुत्वा मुनि वेष धरम् च तम् |
समीक्ष्य सह भार्याभी राजा विगत चेतनः

rāmasya tu vacaḥ śrutvā muni veṣa dharam ca tam |
samīkṣya saha bhāryābhī rājā vigata cetanaḥ

Hearing Rama's words and seeing him clad in a garb of hermit, king Dasaratha with
his wives fell unconscious. Consumed by grief, he could not look towards Rama. Troubled in mind as
he was, the king could not speak to Rama by seeing into his face.

Dasaratha' s palace, which was earlier resounding with tomtoms, large drums and

Sumantra arranges for a chariot for the travel of Rama, Seetha, and Lakshmana at the orders of Dasharatha. Kingly ornaments are obtained for Seetha. Rama seeks blessings from his mother Kausalya and others too. Kausalya's parting advises to Seetha.
