Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 52

As per instructions of Rama, Guha gets a boat ready

As per instructions of Rama, Guha gets a boat ready. Sumantra requests Rama to take him as a personal attendant to the forest, but Rama declines his offer and after reasoning with him, sends him back to Ayodhya.

--***-The river Ganga, when brought down from heaven by the austerities of
Emperor Bhagiratha, followed the latter to the subterranean regions in order to consecrate the

Jahnu, who in his anger, drank up its waters. But the gods and sages and particularly Bhagiratha
appeased his anger and he consented to release the waters through his ears. The river is thereafter
regarded as his daughter.
