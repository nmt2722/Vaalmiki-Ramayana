Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 17

This chapter further describes journey towards the royal palace

This chapter further describes journey towards the royal palace. On the way Rama hears blessings and well meaning words of his friends and various people gathered there.

1Ayodhya Kanda 17.1

स रमो रथमास्थाय सम्प्रहृष्टसुहृञनः |
पताकाध्वजसंपन्नं महार्हगुरुधूपितम्
अपश्यन्नगरं श्रीमान् नानाजनसमाकुलम्

sa rāmaḥ ratham āsthāya samprahRṣṭa suhRj janaḥ |
patākādhvajasaṃpannaṃ mahārhagurudhūpitam
apaśyan nagaram śrīmān nānā jana samākulam

Rama the glorious man, who was with his joyful friends, ascended the chariot and saw
the city which was decorated with flags and banners, which was perfumed all around by burning excellent
aloe wood and which was endowed with varied types of people.

17Ayodhya Kanda 17.17

स राजकुलमासाद्य मेघसंघोपमैः शुभैः

sa rāja kulam āsādya mahā indra bhavana upamam

with blazing radiance like the palace of king of celestials on earth. All around the house, there were
