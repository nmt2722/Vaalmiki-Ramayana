Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 56

Seetha, Rama and Lakshmana reach the mountain of Chitrakuta…

Seetha, Rama and Lakshmana reach the mountain of Chitrakuta, beholding and showing one another the grandeur of the woods there.

1Ayodhya Kanda 56.1

अथ रात्र्याम् व्यतीतायाम् अवसुप्तम् अनन्तरम् |
प्रबोधयाम् आस शनैः लक्ष्मणम् रघु नन्दनः

atha rātryām vyatītāyām avasuptam anantaram |
prabodhayām āsa śanaiḥ lakṣmaṇam raghu nandanaḥ

Then, Rama gently woke up Lakshmana, who was sleeping without break, even when the
night was passing away.

2Ayodhya Kanda 56.2

सौमित्रे शृणु वन्यानाम् वल्गु व्याहरताम् स्वनम् |
सम्प्रतिष्ठामहे कालः प्रस्थानस्य परम् तप

saumitre śṛṇu vanyānām valgu vyāharatām svanam |

"Oh, Lakshmana! Hear the sound of wild animals, resounding beautifully. It is time

3Ayodhya Kanda 56.3

स सुप्तः समये भ्रात्रा लक्ष्मणः प्रतिबोधितः |

4Ayodhya Kanda 56.4

6Ayodhya Kanda 56.6

7Ayodhya Kanda 56.7

8Ayodhya Kanda 56.8

9Ayodhya Kanda 56.9

10Ayodhya Kanda 56.10

11Ayodhya Kanda 56.11

12Ayodhya Kanda 56.12

13Ayodhya Kanda 56.13

14Ayodhya Kanda 56.14

15Ayodhya Kanda 56.15
