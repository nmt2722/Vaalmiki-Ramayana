Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 58

As per the orders of king Dasaratha, Sumantra delivers the parting messages…

As per the orders of king Dasaratha, Sumantra delivers the parting messages of Rama and Lakshmana. Rama sends positive messages to Kausalya and Bharata but Lakshmana sends angry messages to Dasaratha.

"The king even took shelter under a wretched decree of Kaikeyi and performed an act
not to be done, as though it is a proper deed, for which we are now tormented."

27. raajJNaa tu= the king even;aashritya= took shelter under;laghu= the wretched;shaasanam= decree;kaikeyyaaH= of Kaikeyi;kR^itam= and did;akaaryam= an act not to be done;kaaryam= as though it is a proper act;yena= by which;vayam= we;abhipiiDitaH= are tormented.

28Ayodhya Kanda 58.28

यदि प्रव्राजितः रामः लोभ कारण कारितम् |
वर दान निमित्तम् वा सर्वथा दुष्कृतम् कृतम्

yadi pravrājitaḥ rāmaḥ lobha kāraṇa kāritam |
vara dāna nimittam vā sarvathā duṣkṛtam kṛtam

"Even if this was done for the good pleasure of the king or through the will of God,
I do not see any justification for Rama's abandonment."

28. lobha kaaraNa kaaritam= either instigated by passion;vara daana nimittam vaa= or with the intention of giving boons;raamaH= Rama;pravraajitaH yadi= was sent to exile;dushhkR^itam= an infamous deed;kR^itam= was done;sarvathaa= by all means."

29Ayodhya Kanda 58.29

इदम् तावद्यथाकाममीश्वरस्य कृते कृतम् |
रामस्य तु परित्यागे न हेतुम् उपलक्षये

idam tāvadyathākāmamīśvarasya kṛte kṛtam |

30Ayodhya Kanda 58.30

असमीक्ष्य समारब्धम् विरुद्धम् बुद्धि लाघवात् |
जनयिष्यति सम्क्रोशम् राघवस्य विवासनम्

asamīkṣya samārabdham viruddham buddhi lāghavāt |
janayiṣyati samkrośam rāghavasya vivāsanam

"Rama's exile, an illegal decision undertaken whether due to levity of judgment or

30. raaghavasya= Rama's;vivaasam= exile;viruddham= an illegal decision;samaarabdham= undertaken;buddhi laaghavaat= whether due to levity of judgment;asamiikshhya= or due to lack of reflection;janayishhyati= will raise;samkrosham= intensive anguish.

31Ayodhya Kanda 58.31

अहम् तावन् महा राजे पितृत्वम् न उपलक्षये |
भ्राता भर्ता च बन्धुः च पिता च मम राघवः

aham tāvan mahā rāje pitṛtvam na upalakṣaye |
bhrātā bhartā ca bandhuḥ ca pitā ca mama rāghavaḥ

"I do not consider the emperor as my father. For me, Rama is the brother, lord,

32Ayodhya Kanda 58.32

सर्व लोक प्रियम् त्यक्त्वा सर्व लोक हिते रतम् |
सर्व लोको अनुरज्येत कथम् त्वा अनेन कर्मणा

sarva loka priyam tyaktvā sarva loka hite ratam |
sarva loko anurajyeta katham tvā anena karmaṇā

"Leaving Rama the beloved of all people and who is interested in the welfare of all

33Ayodhya Kanda 58.33

सर्वप्रजाभिरामम् हि रामम् प्रव्राज्य धार्मिकम् |
सर्वलोकम् विरुध्येमम् कथम् राजा भविष्यसि

34Ayodhya Kanda 58.34

जानकी तु महा राज निःश्वसन्ती तपस्विनी |
भूत उपहत चित्ता इव विष्ठिता वृष्मृता स्थिता

jānakī tu mahā rāja niḥśvasantī tapasvinī |
bhūta upahata cittā iva viṣṭhitā vṛṣmṛtā sthitā

35Ayodhya Kanda 58.35

अदृष्ट पूर्व व्यसना राज पुत्री यशस्विनी |
तेन दुह्खेन रुदती न एव माम् किंचित् अब्रवीत्

36Ayodhya Kanda 58.36

उद्वीक्षमाणा भर्तारम् मुखेन परिशुष्यता |
मुमोच सहसा बाष्पम् माम् प्रयान्तम् उदीक्ष्य सा

37Ayodhya Kanda 58.37

तथैव रामः अश्रु मुखः कृत अन्जलिः |
स्थितः अभवल् लक्ष्मण बाहु पालितः स्थितः |
तथैव सीता रुदती तपस्विनी |
निरीक्षते राज रथम् तथैव माम्

tathaiva rāmaḥ aśru mukhaḥ kṛta anjaliḥ |

"In the same way, Rama with tears on his face and with joined palms, stood being

the royal chariot and me."

37. tathaiva= In the same way;raamaH= Rama;ashru mukhaH= with tears on his face;kR^ita anjaliH= and with joined palms;sthitaH abhaval= stood;lakSmaNa baahu paalitaH= shielded by the arms of Lakshmana;tathaiva= In the same way;tapasvinii= the miserable;siitaa= Seetha;rudatii= weeping;niriikSate= was seeing;raaja ratham= the royal chariot;tathaiva= and;maam= me."

As per the orders of king Dasaratha, Sumantra delivers the parting messages of Rama and Lakshmana. Rama sends positive messages to Kausalya and Bharata but Lakshmana sends angry messages to Dasaratha.
