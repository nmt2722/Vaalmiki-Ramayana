Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 1

Valmiki describes the innumerable virtues of Sree Rama in this sarga

Valmiki describes the innumerable virtues of Sree Rama in this sarga. Dasaratha wants to coronate Rama as the crown prince with the approval of all his people.

10Ayodhya Kanda 1.10

11Ayodhya Kanda 1.11

12Ayodhya Kanda 1.12

13Ayodhya Kanda 1.13

बुद्धिमान्मधुराभाषी पूर्वभाषी प्रियंवदः |

14Ayodhya Kanda 1.14
