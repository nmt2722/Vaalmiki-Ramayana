Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 82

Vasishta requests Bharata to get himself anointed as a king

Vasishta requests Bharata to get himself anointed as a king. Bharata refuses to rob the throne from Rama.

1Ayodhya Kanda 82.1

ताम् आर्य गण सम्पूर्णाम् भरतः प्रग्रहाम् सभाम् |

11Ayodhya Kanda 82.11

चरित ब्रह्मचर्यस्य विद्या स्नातस्य धीमतः |

13Ayodhya Kanda 82.13

Vasishta requests Bharata to get himself anointed as a king. Bharata refuses to rob the throne from Rama. Who is really elighible for it as the eldest son of the family.
