Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 71

Spending seven nights in his journey, crossing various streams and rivers…

Spending seven nights in his journey, crossing various streams and rivers and passing through many woods and territories, Bharata reaches the city of Ayodhya, from there ,he finds the city of Ayodhya in a forlorn mood and discusses this situation…
