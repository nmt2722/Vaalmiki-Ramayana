Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 7

Manthara feels very sad after hearing about anointment of Rama…

Manthara feels very sad after hearing about anointment of Rama for the princely kingdom. She tells Kaikeyi about the coronation of Rama. Kaikeyi becomes delighted and gives Manthara one of her jewels.

28Ayodhya Kanda 7.28

यथा हि कुर्यात्सर्पो वा शत्रुर्वा प्रत्युपेक्षितः |
राज्ञा दशरथेनाद्य सपुत्रा त्वं तथा कृता

yathā hi kuryātsarpo vā śatrurvā pratyupekṣitaḥ |
rājñā daśarathenādya saputrā tvaṃ tathā kṛtā

"King Dasaratha has acted towards you and your son today in the same way as an enemy

29Ayodhya Kanda 7.29

पापेनानृतसान्त्वेन बाले नित्यं सुखोचिते |
रामं स्थापयता राज्ये सानुबन्धा हता ह्यसि

pāpenānṛtasāntvena bāle nityaṃ sukhocite |
rāmaṃ sthāpayatā rājye sānubandhā hatā hyasi

"O, stupid one! With his untruthful sweet words to you, who are ever deserving of

30Ayodhya Kanda 7.30

सा प्राप्तकालं कैकेयि क्षिप्रं कुरु हितं तव |
त्रायस्व पुत्रमात्मानं मां च विस्मयदर्शने

sā prāptakālaṃ kaikeyi kṣipraṃ kuru hitaṃ tava |

31Ayodhya Kanda 7.31

मन्थराया वचः श्रुत्वा शयनात्स शुभानना |
उत्तस्थौ हर्षसंपूर्णा चन्द्रलेखेव शारदी

mantharāyā vacaḥ śrutvā śayanātsa śubhānanā |
uttasthau harṣasaṃpūrṇā candralekheva śāradī

32Ayodhya Kanda 7.32

एकमाभरणं तस्यै कुब्जायै प्रददौ शुभम्

33Ayodhya Kanda 7.33

34Ayodhya Kanda 7.34

इदं तु मन्थरे मह्यमाख्यासि परमं प्रियम् |
एतन्मे प्रियमाख्यातुः किं वा भूयः करोमि ते

35Ayodhya Kanda 7.35

रामे वा भरते वाहं विशेषं नोपलक्षये |
तस्मात्तुष्टास्मि यद्राजा रामं राज्येऽभिषेक्ष्यति

tasmāttuṣṭāsmi yadrājā rāmaṃ rājye.abhiṣekṣyati

36Ayodhya Kanda 7.36

न मे परम् किञ्चि दितस्त्वयापि न |
प्रियं प्रियार्हे सुवचम् वचो परम् |
तथा ह्यवोचस्त्वमतः प्रियोत्तरं |
वरं वरं ते प्रददामि तं वृणु

na me param kiñci ditastvayāpi na |
priyaṃ priyārhe suvacam vaco param |
tathā hyavocastvamataḥ priyottaraṃ |

"O, Manthara! You do merit every favor. I have no other pleasant news than this.
Hence, you cannot ever again inform a better loving news than this. I shall give you a good boon.
Ask for it!"

36. priyaarhe= O;

Manthara feels very sad after hearing about anointment of Rama for the princely kingdom. She tells Kaikeyi about the coronation of Rama. Kaikeyi becomes delighted and gives Manthara one of her jewels.
