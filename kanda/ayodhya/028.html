1Ayodhya Kanda 28.1

सएवम् ब्रुवतीम् सीताम् धर्मज्ञो धर्म वत्सलः |
निवर्तन अर्थे धर्म आत्मा वाक्यम् एतत् उवाच ह

saevam bruvatīm sītām dharmajño dharma vatsalaḥ |
nivartana arthe dharma ātmā vākyam etat uvāca ha

Thinking of difficulties one will be called upon to bear in the forest, Rama who was
fond of righteousness did not form the idea of taking Sita who was speaking as aforesaid.

3Ayodhya Kanda 28.3

4Ayodhya Kanda 28.4

5Ayodhya Kanda 28.5

6Ayodhya Kanda 28.6

7Ayodhya Kanda 28.7

8Ayodhya Kanda 28.8

9Ayodhya Kanda 28.9

10Ayodhya Kanda 28.10

11Ayodhya Kanda 28.11

12Ayodhya Kanda 28.12
