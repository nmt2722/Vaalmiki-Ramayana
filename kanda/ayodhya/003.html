Ayodhya Kanda · Sarga 3

Dasaratha, glad after hearing the words of assembled people…

Dasaratha, glad after hearing the words of assembled people, requests Vasishta and Vamadeva to start arrangements for the anointing ceremony of Rama.

4Ayodhya Kanda 3.4

caitraḥ śrīmānayaṃ māsaḥ puṇyaḥ puṣpitakānanaḥ |
