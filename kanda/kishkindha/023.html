Kishkindha Kanda · Sarga 23

Tara's lamentation is narrated

Tara's lamentation is narrated in here. Seeing at her departed husband Vali, Tara pitiably wails for his demise as any ordinary woman would.

1Kishkindha Kanda 23.1

ततः समुपजिघ्रंती कपि राजस्य तत् मुखम् |
पतिम् लोकश्रुता तारा मृतम् वचनम् अब्रवीत्

tataḥ samupajighraṃtī kapi rājasya tat mukham |

2Kishkindha Kanda 23.2

उपल उपचिते वीर सुदुःखे वसुधा तले

3Kishkindha Kanda 23.3

मत्तः प्रियतरा नूनम् वानरेन्द्र मही तव |
शेषे हि ताम् परिष्वज्य माम् च न प्रतिभाषसे

mattaḥ priyatarā nūnam vānarendra mahī tava |
śeṣe hi tām pariṣvajya mām ca na pratibhāṣase

4Kishkindha Kanda 23.4

सुग्रीवस्य वशम् प्राप्तो विधिः एष भवत्य अहो |
सुग्रीव एव विक्रांतो वीर साहसिक प्रिय

5Kishkindha Kanda 23.5

ऋक्ष वानर मुख्याः त्वाम् बलिनम् पर्युपासते |
तेषाम् विलपितम् कृच्छ्रम् अंगदस्य च शोचतः
मम च इमा गिरः श्रुत्वा किम् त्वम् न प्रतिबुध्यसे

ṛkṣa vānara mukhyāḥ tvām balinam paryupāsate |
teṣām vilapitam kṛcchram aṃgadasya ca śocataḥ
mama ca imā giraḥ śrutvā kim tvam na pratibudhyase

"These chiefs of bears and monkeys are adoring you in every respect as their most worshipful one, and
on hearing their despairing wailing, and the lamentation of Angada, and even these utterance of mine
in keen, why do not you comeback to senses. [3-23-5, 6a]

5, 6a. R^ikSa vaanara mukhyaaH= bears, monkeys, chiefs;balinam= as worshipful one [not lexical mighty];tvaam pari upaasate= [you as the] mightiest one, you, wholly, are adoring;teSaam kR^icChram vilapitam= their, despairing, wailing;shocataH angadasya ca= lamentation, of Angada, also;mama imaa giraH ca= mine, these, utterances [in keen];shrutvaa= on hearing;kim tvam na= why, you, not;prati budhyase= in turn, wake up, come to senses.

6Kishkindha Kanda 23.6

इदम् तत् वीर शयनम् तत्र शेषे हतो युधि
शायिता निहता यत्र त्वया एव रिपवः पुरा

idam tat vīra śayanam tatra śeṣe hato yudhi
śāyitā nihatā yatra tvayā eva ripavaḥ purā

"Do you wish to repose on the very daybed on which you have laid your enemies to rest when you have

7Kishkindha Kanda 23.7

विशुद्ध सत्त्व अभिजन प्रिययुद्ध मम प्रिय
माम् अनाथाम् विहाय एकाम् गतः त्वम् असि मानद

viśuddha sattva abhijana priyayuddha mama priya
mām anāthām vihāya ekām gataḥ tvam asi
mānada

"Oh, successor of a clan with pristine calibre, oh, accorder of dignity, oh, devotee of fighting, oh,

8Kishkindha Kanda 23.8

शूराय न प्रदातव्या कन्या खलु विपश्चिता
शूर भार्याम् हताम् पश्य सद्यो माम् विधवाम् कृताम्

śūrāya na pradātavyā kanyā khalu vipaścitā
śūra bhāryām hatām paśya sadyo mām vidhavām
kṛtām

"Really considerate fathers shall never propose brides to adventurers, they say. Yes! See me the wife
of an adventurer, in a trice rendered as a war-widow left in the lurch. [3-23-8b, 9a]

8b, 9a. vi pashcitaa= by really, considerate ones [father of brides];kanyaa= brides;shuuraaya na pradaatavyaa khalu= to adventurers, not, to be proposed, they say;sadyaH= immediately, in a trice;vi dhavaam kR^itaam= without, husband, made [widow, rendered as];hataam= left in the lurch;shuura bhaaryaam= adventurer's, wife;maam pashya= me, see.

For thisSage Kaatyaanasays :duurasthaanaam avidyaanaam mokSha maarga anusaariNaam |
shuuraaNaam nir dhanaanaam ca na deyaa kanyakaa budhaiH ||'to those bridegrooms who reside in distant
places, uneducated, seekers of salvation, daring ones, un-wealthy brides shall not be given by wisemen...'

9Kishkindha Kanda 23.9

अवभग्नः च मे मानो भग्ना मे शाश्वती गतिः
अगाधे च निमग्ना अस्मि विपुले शोक सागरे

avabhagnaḥ ca me māno bhagnā me śāśvatī gatiḥ
agādhe ca nimagnā asmi vipule śoka sāgare

10Kishkindha Kanda 23.10

अश्म सारमयम् नूनम् इदम् मे हृदयम् दृढम्
भर्तारम् निहतम् दृष्ट्वा यत् न अद्य शतधा गतम्

aśma sāramayam nūnam idam me hṛdayam dṛḍham
bhartāram nihatam dṛṣṭvā yat na adya śatadhā
gatam

"And dense with metallic substance is this heart of mine in its solidity, since it is not splintering

10b, 11a. nihatam bhartaaram dR^iSTvaa= killed, husband, on seeing;yat= which [heart];adya shatadhaa na gatam [kR^itam]= now, in hundredfold, not, gone into [splintered];[tat= that heart];me dR^iDham idam hR^idayam= mine, solid, this, heart;ashma saara mayam= metallic, substance, dense with;nuunam= definitely.

11Kishkindha Kanda 23.11

सुहृत् चैव हि भर्ता च प्रकृत्या च मम प्रियः
प्रहारे च पराक्रान्तः शूरः पंचत्वम् आगतः

suhṛt caiva hi bhartā ca prakṛtyā ca mama priyaḥ
prahāre ca parākrāntaḥ śūraḥ paṃcatvam
āgataḥ

"By your nature you are my beloved, besides, you are a good-hearted one, more so, you are my husband,

11b, 12a mama= to me prakR^ityaa priyaH = by nature, beloved one;su hR^it caiva= good, hearted one, also thus;bhartaa ca= husband, also;prahaare para aakraantaH ca= in assaults, others - enemies, overcoming [triumphant one];shuuraH= valiant one;

12Kishkindha Kanda 23.12

पति हीना तु या नारी कामम् भवतु पुत्रिणी
धन धान्य समृद्धा अपि विधवा इति उच्यते जनैः

pati hīnā tu yā nārī kāmam bhavatu putriṇī
dhana dhānya samṛddhā api vidhavā iti ucyate
janaiḥ

"Perhaps a woman might have mothered many children, and perhaps affluent with riches and crops, still

13Kishkindha Kanda 23.13

स्व गात्र प्रभवे वीर शेषे रुधिर मण्डले

sva gātra prabhave vīra śeṣe rudhira maṇḍale
kṛmi rāga paristome svakīye śayane yathā

"How do you repose now on a sheet of blood flowing from your own body, oh, brave one, as if you are

14Kishkindha Kanda 23.14

रेणु शोणित संवीतम् गात्रम् तव समंततः

15Kishkindha Kanda 23.15

कृत कृत्यो अद्य सुग्रीवो वैरे अस्मिन् अतिदारुणे
यस्य राम विमुक्तेन हृतम् एक इषुणा भयम्

yasya rāma vimuktena hṛtam eka iṣuṇā bhayam

16Kishkindha Kanda 23.16

शरेण हृदि लग्नेन गात्र संस्पर्शने तव
वार्यामि त्वाम् निरीक्षन्ती त्वयि पंचत्वम् आगते

śareṇa hṛdi lagnena gātra saṃsparśane tava
vāryāmi tvām nirīkṣantī tvayi paṃcatvam āgate

"In vain, I have been watching you without a wink, on whom fatality has descended, as this arrow stuck

17Kishkindha Kanda 23.17

उद्बबर्ह शरम् नीलः तस्य गात्र गतम् तदा
गिरि गह्वर संलीनम् दीप्तम् आशी विषम् यथा

udbabarha śaram nīlaḥ tasya gātra gatam tadā
giri gahvara saṃlīnam dīptam āśī viṣam yathā

"Then the monkey chief Nila extricated that arrow which is stuck in the chest of Vali, as with the extraction

Nila;tasya gaatra gatam sharam= his [Valis's,] body - chest, gone in [stuck in,] arrow;giri gahvara= in mountain's, cavity;sam liinam= firmly, wedged in;diiptam= sparkling [arrow];aashiiviSam yathaa= serpent [like arrow,] as with;ut babarha= out, pulled - extricated.

18Kishkindha Kanda 23.18

तस्य निष्कृष्यमाणस्य बाणस्य च बभौ द्युतिः
अस्त मस्तक संरुद्धो रश्मिः दिनकरात् इव

tasya niṣkṛṣyamāṇasya bāṇasya ca babhau dyutiḥ
asta mastaka saṃruddho raśmiḥ dinakarāt
iva

The sparkle of Rama's arrow while it is being extracted from Vali's chest is much the same as the sparkles
of more brilliant sunrays that are extracted by the end of daytime, when the sun is sinking beyond the
summit of dusky western mountain, and as contrasted with the hue of dusk. [3-23-18b, 19a]

18b, 19a. niSkR^iSyamaaNasya= while being extracted;tasya baaNasya dyutiH= that, arrow's, sparkle;dinakaraat= from sun - that which is extricated from sun;asta mastaka= dusk-time [western mountain's,] summit;sam ruddhaH= fully, obstructed - as contrasted with [the hue of dusk];rashmiH iva= sparkling rays, like;babhau= it became - shone forth.

More brilliant days are indicated for the arrows of Rama as the dusky day of Vali has ended.

19Kishkindha Kanda 23.19

पेतुः क्षतज धाराः तु व्रणेभ्यः तस्य सर्वशः

20Kishkindha Kanda 23.20

अवकीर्णम् विमार्जन्ती भर्तारम् रण रेणुना

21Kishkindha Kanda 23.21

रुधिरोक्षित सर्वान्गम् दृष्ट्वा विनिहतम् पतिम्

22Kishkindha Kanda 23.22

अवस्थाम् पश्चिमाम् पश्य पितुः पुत्र सुदारुणाम्

23Kishkindha Kanda 23.23

बाल सूर्योज्ज्वल तनुम् प्रयातम् यम सादनम्

24Kishkindha Kanda 23.24

एवम् उक्तः समुत्थाय जग्राह चरणौ पितुः
भुजाभ्याम् पीन वृताभ्याम् अंगदो अहम् इति ब्रुवन्

evam uktaḥ samutthāya jagrāha caraṇau pituḥ
bhujābhyām pīna vṛtābhyām aṃgado aham iti
bruvan

When he is said so, Angada swiftly rose and touched the feet of his father with both of his rotund-shouldered
sinewy arms saying, "father, I am Angada..." [3-23-24b, 25a]

24b, 25a. evam uktaH= thus, who is said to, Angada;sam utthaaya= swiftly, on rising up;aham angadaH= I am, Angada;iti bruvan= thus, saying;piina vR^itaabhyaam= with sinewy, spherical shoulders;bhujaabhyaam= with arms;pituH caraNau jagraaha= father's, feet, he took.

25Kishkindha Kanda 23.25

अभिवादयमानम् त्वाम् अंगदम् त्वम् यथा पुरा
दीर्घ आयुर् भव पुत्र इति किम् अर्थम् न अभिभाषसे

abhivādayamānam tvām aṃgadam tvam yathā purā
dīrgha āyur bhava putra iti kim artham na
abhibhāṣase

"Why do not you bless Angada saying, 'long live, son...' when he is paying resects to you, as earlier?"
Thus Tara is addressing Vali in her anguish. [3-23-25b, 26a]

25b, 26a. tvaam= at you;abhivaadayamaanam= to him who is paying respects;angadam= at Angada;tvam= you;putra= oh, son;diirgha aayuH bhava= long, live, you be;iti= thus as;kim artham= what, for - why don't you;yathaa puraa= as, earlier;abhibhaaSase= not, speaking [blessing.]

26Kishkindha Kanda 23.26

अहम् पुत्र सहाया त्वाम् उपासे गत चेतनम् |
सिंहेन पातितम् सद्यो गौः स वत्सा इव गो वृषम्

aham putra sahāyā tvām upāse gata cetanam |
siṃhena pātitam sadyo gauḥ sa vatsā iva go vṛṣam

"I am living through seeing the lifeless state of yours with the help of my son, no more than a helpless
cow that sits near her bull with its calf, when that bull is slain by a lion, just now. [3-23-26b, c]

26b, c. sadyaH= just now;simhena paatitam= lion, felled;go vR^iSam= cow, male [when bull is felled];sa gauH vatsaa iva= with, calf, [a helpless] cow, as like;putra sahaayaa= son, with the help of;aham= I;gata cetanam= [ you who] lost, vitality - lifeless;tvaam= to you [at your near];upaase= sitting close - living through.

27Kishkindha Kanda 23.27

इष्ट्वा संग्राम यज्ञेन राम प्रहरण अंभसा |
अस्मिन् अवभृथे स्नातः कथम् पत्न्या मया विना

iṣṭvā saṃgrāma yajñena rāma praharaṇa aṃbhasā |
asmin avabhṛthe snātaḥ katham patnyā mayā vinā ||
3-23-27

"How can you singly take the ultimate bath of a Vedic ritual after your conducting a ritual like combat,
in the waters called bloodstreams caused by Rama's arrow, that too, when I, your wife, am available like
a co-officiator of a Vedic ritual? [3-23-27]

27. sangraama yaj~nena iSTvaa= war, ritual, on performing;patnyaa mayaa vinaa= wife, me, without;raama praharaNa ambhasaa= Rama's, weapon [arrow,] [called] waters;asmin avabhR^ithe= in there, avabridtha, [ritual bath];katham snaataH= how, you take bath [singly.]

ThisavabR^ithais a religious bath taken after completion of the Vedic ritual by the conductor of that
ritual along with his wife. Vedic ritual itself is forbidden to a wifeless person, and taking this final
bath afterpuurNa aahuti, total oblation, is against the rules of ritual if taken without his wife.
Here Vali, an adorer of combats as good as Vedic rituals is taking that bath for himself in the bloodstreams,
called ritual waters, and Tara is questioning him about her propriety to participate in it.

29Kishkindha Kanda 23.29

राज्यश्रीः न जहाति त्वाम् गत असुम् अपि मानद |
सूर्यस्य आवर्तमानस्य शैल राजम् इव प्रभा

rājyaśrīḥ na jahāti tvām gata asum api mānada |
sūryasya āvartamānasya śaila rājam iva prabhā ||
3-23-29

"Majesty is not deserting you even if your lives have departed, oh, accorder of dignity, as with the
sunshine that departs the sun while he circumnavigates Mt. Meru, which mount is supposed to overshadow
everything by its vastness. [3-23-29]

29. maana da= dignity, accorder;shaila raajam aavartamaanasya= mountain, best [Mt. Meru,] circumnavigating;suuryasya prabhaa iva= sun's, shine, as with;raajya shriiH= kingly, pomp [majesty];gata asum api= one with gone, lives, even if;tvaam na jahaati= you, not, leaving.

30Kishkindha Kanda 23.30

न मे वचः पथ्यम् इदम् त्वया कृतम्
न च अस्मि शक्ता हि निवारणे तव |
हता सपुत्रा अस्मि हतेन संयुगे
सह त्वया श्रीः विजहाति माम् अपि

na me vacaḥ pathyam idam tvayā kṛtam
na ca asmi śaktā hi nivāraṇe tava |
hatā saputrā asmi hatena
saṃyuge
saha tvayā śrīḥ vijahāti mām api

"Neither you heeded my word of expediency, nor I was indeed capable to forestall you, and I am doomed
along with my son when you are exterminated in fight, whereby the Grace that forsook you, is deserting
me too, in its entirety. [3-23-30]

30. tvayaa= by you;me= my;pathyam idam vacaH= expedient, this, words [my advise];na kR^itam= not, done [heeded];tava nivaaraNe= you, forestalling;shaktaa na asmi ca hi= capable, not, I am, also, indeed;samyuge hatena= in fight, by you who is killed;sa putraa= along with, son;hataa asmi= doomed, I am;shriiH= Grace;tvayaa saha= you, along with;maam api= me, even;vi jahaati= completely, deserting.

Tara's lamentation is narrated in here. Seeing at her departed husband Vali, Tara pitiably wails for his demise as any ordinary woman would.
