किष्किन्धाकाण्ड

Kishkindha Kanda

The Book of Kishkindha

The alliance. Rama meets Hanuman and befriends Sugriva, the exiled vanara king. Vali is killed, Sugriva is crowned, and the vanara hosts are summoned from every quarter. When Sita is traced to Lanka, Hanuman leaps the ocean to find her.
