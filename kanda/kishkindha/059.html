Kishkindha Kanda · Sarga 59

Sampaati details his narration telling monkeys as to how he knew…

Sampaati details his narration telling monkeys as to how he knew the abduction of Seetha. He says that his son Supaarshva is the eyewitness in her abduction and narrates that account to monkeys, which his son narrated to him.

1Kishkindha Kanda 59.1

9Kishkindha Kanda 59.9

तीक्ष्ण कामाः तु गंधर्वाः तीक्ष्ण कोपा भुजंगमाः |

10Kishkindha Kanda 59.10

18Kishkindha Kanda 59.18

Some other mms contain the wordingdiSTyaa jiivasi taatawheretaata'my boy...'

24Kishkindha Kanda 59.24

वाक् मतिभ्याम् हि सार्वेषाम् करिष्यामि प्रियम् हि वः
यत् हि दाशरथेः कार्यम् मम तत् न अत्र संशयः

vāk matibhyām hi sārveṣām kariṣyāmi priyam hi vaḥ
yat hi dāśaratheḥ kāryam mama tat na atra saṃśayaḥ

"Indeed with my analysis and information to you I wish to make all of you exuberant
as I deem that I have done my bit, because whatever task of Rama, the son of Dasharatha, is there
that task is mine too. There is no doubt bout it. [4-59-24b, 25a]

24b, 25a. saarveSaam vaH= to all, of you;vaak matibhyaam priyam kariSyaami hi= with words [information,] mind [ analysis,] exuberant, I wish to make [you,] indeed;yat daasharatheH kaaryam= which is, of Rama of Dasharatha, task;tat mama= that [task,] is mine [too];na atra samshayaH= no, in that matter, doubt.

25Kishkindha Kanda 59.25

तत् भवन्तो मति श्रेष्ठा बलवन्तो मनस्विनः
प्रहिताः कपि राजेन देवैः अपि दुरासदाः

tat bhavanto mati śreṣṭhā balavanto manasvinaḥ
prahitāḥ kapi rājena devaiḥ api durāsadāḥ

"Thereby, I reckon you as the best ones by your aptitude, fortitude and attitude,
and as invincible ones even for gods, hence Sugreeva, the king of monkeys, shepherded you...
[4-59-25, 26a]

25b, 26a. tat= thereby;mati shreSThaa= in aptitude, best ones;manasvinaH= good at attitude;balavantaH= having fortitude;devaiH api duraasadaaH= by gods, even, invincible ones;bhavantaH= such as you are;kapi raajena= by monkeys, king Sugreeva;prahitaaH= you are shepherded.

26Kishkindha Kanda 59.26

राम लक्ष्मण बाणाः च निशिताः कंक पत्रिणः
त्रयाणाम् अपि लोकानाम् पर्याप्ताः त्राण निग्रहे

rāma lakṣmaṇa bāṇāḥ ca niśitāḥ kaṃka patriṇaḥ
trayāṇām api lokānām paryāptāḥ trāṇa nigrahe

"What is more, the excruciating eagle-feathered arrows of Rama and Lakshmana are
enough to give defence or offence to all the three worlds. [4-59-26b, 27a]

26b, 27a. kanka patriNaH= eagle, feathered ones;nishitaaH= excruciating ones;raama lakSmaNa baaNaaH ca= by Rama's, by Lakshmana's, arrows, what is more;trayaaNaam lokaanaam api= three, worlds, even for;traaNa nigrahe= for defending, and for offending;paryaaptaaH= are enough.

27Kishkindha Kanda 59.27

कामम् खलु दशग्रीवः तेजो बल समन्वितः |
भवताम् तु समर्थानाम् न किंचित् अपि दुष्करम्

kāmam khalu daśagrīvaḥ tejo bala samanvitaḥ |
bhavatām tu samarthānām na kiṃcit api duṣkaram

"Perchance that Decahedral demon is definitely a powerful and prideful demon. But,
as capable vanara-s there is nothing impossible to you, even in the least. [4-59-27]

27b, c. dashagriivaH= Decahedral demon;tejaH bala samanvitaH khalu kaamam= power, pride, possessor of, definitely, perchance;tu= but;samarthaanaam bhavataam= capable ones, by you;duSkaram= impossible;kimcit api= in the least, even;na= is not there.

28Kishkindha Kanda 59.28

तत् अलम् काल संगेन क्रियताम् बुद्धि निश्चयः |
न हि कर्मसु सज्जन्ते बुद्धिमन्तो भवत् विधाः

tat alam kāla saṃgena kriyatām buddhi niścayaḥ |
na hi karmasu sajjante buddhimanto bhavat vidhāḥ

"Thereby, enough is the time-lapse and make a mental determination. Adventurers and

29Kishkindha Kanda 59.29

The source page does not carry a separate English translation for this shloka. The Devanagari and IAST are complete.

Sampaati details his narration telling monkeys as to how he knew the abduction of Seetha. He says that his son Supaarshva is the eyewitness in her abduction and narrates that account to monkeys, which his son narrated to him.
