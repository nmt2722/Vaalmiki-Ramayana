Sundara Kanda · Sarga 16

Hanuma is grief stricken seeing the plight of Seetha…

Hanuma is grief stricken seeing the plight of Seetha, in that garden being surrounded by ogresses. He becomes firm in mind that She is indeed Seetha, based on Her auspicious bodily marks.

1Sundara Kanda 16.1

इमाम् असित केश अन्ताम् शत पत्र निभ ईक्षणाम् |
सुख अर्हाम् दुःखिताम् ज्~आत्वा मम अपि व्यथितम्
मनः

imām asita keśa antām śata patra nibha īkṣaṇām |

29Sundara Kanda 16.29

क्षिति क्षमा पुष्कर सम्निभ अक्षी |

ईक्षणाभिः |

"Whoever Seetha with earth-like patience, with eyes resembling lotuses, was protected by Sri Rama

30Sundara Kanda 16.30

हिम हत नलिनी इव नष्ट शोभा |
व्यसन परम्परया निपीड्यमाना |
सह चर रहिता इव चक्र वाकी |

31Sundara Kanda 16.31

अस्या हि पुष्प अवनत अग्र शाखाः |
शोकम् दृढम् वै जनयति अशोकाः |
हिम व्यपायेन च शीतरश्मिः
|
रभ्युत्थितो न एक सहस्र रश्मिः

asyā hi puṣpa avanata agra śākhāḥ |
śokam dṛḍham vai janayati aśokāḥ |
hima vyapāyena
ca śītaraśmiḥ |
rabhyutthito na eka sahasra raśmiḥ

"Ashoka trees with bent branches due to flowers are giving rise to gloom a lot to Her; Due to melting

32Sundara Kanda 16.32

इति एवम् अर्थम् कपिर् अन्ववेक्ष्य |
सीता इयम् इति एव निविष्ट बुद्धिः |
संश्रित्य तस्मिन्
निषसाद वृक्षे |
बली हरीणाम् ऋषभः तरस्वी

iti evam artham kapir anvavekṣya |
sītā iyam iti eva niviṣṭa buddhiḥ |
saṃśritya tasmin
niṣasāda vṛkṣe |
balī harīṇām ṛṣabhaḥ tarasvī

The mighty one, the best among vanaras, quick one, Hanuma thus observed the subject: "This is Seetha!"
- thus with a firm mind sat at that tree, leaning against it.

32. balii= the mighty one;hariiNaam R^iSabhaH= the best among vanaras;tarasvii= quick one;kapiH= Hanuma;iti evam= thus;anvavekSya= observed;artham= the subject;iyam siitaa= this is Seetha;iti eva= thus;niviSTa buddhiH= with a firm mind;niSasaada= sat;tasmin vR^ikSe= at that tree;samshritya= leaning against (it).

Hanuma is grief stricken seeing the plight of Seetha, in that garden being surrounded by ogresses. He becomes firm in mind that She is indeed Seetha, based on Her auspicious bodily marks.
