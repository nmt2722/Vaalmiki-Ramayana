Kanda V

सुन्दरकाण्ड

Sundara Kanda

The Book of Beauty

The beauty of Hanuman. Alone in Lanka, Hanuman searches the city, finds Sita in the Ashoka grove, and carries her message. Captured and condemned, he sets Lanka ablaze with his burning tail, then returns across the sea with news that gives Rama hope.
