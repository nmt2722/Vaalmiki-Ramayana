Sundara Kanda · Sarga 54

Hanuma, with his blazing tail comes out and flits over the horses in Lanka…

Hanuma, with his blazing tail comes out and flits over the horses in Lanka, making up his mind to set fire to the city of Lanka which is the only work let for him to do.

There, all the celestial musicians, Vidyadharas the supernatural beings, Nagas the celestial
serpents, Yakshas the spirits and all great living beings attained a great joy.

50. tatra= there;sarve= all;devaashcha= the celestials;munipuN^gavaashcha= excellent sages;gandharva vidyaadhara naagayakSaaH= Gandharvas the celestials musicians;
Vidyaadharas the supernatural beings;
Nagas the celestial serpents and Yakshas the spirits;sarvaani= all;mahaanti bhuutaani= great living beings;jagmuH= got;paraam= a great;atulya ruupaam= peerless;priitim= delight.

Hanuma, with his blazing tail comes out and flits over the horses in Lanka, making up his mind to set fire to the city of Lanka which is the only work let for him to do. Hanuma burns the entire city, barring the abode of Vibhishana. All the demons were frightened o seeing the blazing fire, consuming their city with its trees, houses and a host of living beings. Surprised to see the city burning, the celestials and musicians (Gandharvas) gain a great delight.
