1Sundara Kanda 23.1

इत्युक्त्वा मैथिलीं राजा रावणः शत्रुरावणः |
संदिश्य च ततः सर्वा राक्षसीर्निर्जगाम ह ||
५-२३-१

ityuktvā maithilīṃ rājā rāvaṇaḥ śatrurāvaṇaḥ |

The king Ravana who makes enemies to cry thus speaking to Seetha thereafter also ordering all ogre
women went away.

2Sundara Kanda 23.2

3Sundara Kanda 23.3

6Sundara Kanda 23.6

7Sundara Kanda 23.7

8Sundara Kanda 23.8
