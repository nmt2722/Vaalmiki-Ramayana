Sundara Kanda · Sarga 56

Hanuma again visits Ashoka garden and sees Seetha

Hanuma again visits Ashoka garden and sees Seetha once more. He consoles Seetha that Rama along with his forces of monkeys and bears will come soon and after conquering the enemies in battle, will take her back.

निपेतुः भू तले रुग्णाः शक्र आयुध हता इव

28Sundara Kanda 56.28

29Sundara Kanda 56.29

त्रस्त व्याअविद्ध वसना व्याकुली कृत भूषणा

32Sundara Kanda 56.32

स च भूमि धरः श्रीमान् बलिना तेन पीडितः
सवृक्ष शिखर उदग्राः प्रविवेश रसा तलम्

33Sundara Kanda 56.33

दश योजन विस्तारः त्रिंशत् योजनम् उच्छ्रितः
धरण्याम् समताम् यातः स बभूव धरा धरः

daśa yojana vistāraḥ triṃśat yojanam ucchritaḥ

34Sundara Kanda 56.34

स लिलङ्घुयिर्भीमम् सलीलम् लवणार्णवम्
कल्लोलास्फालवेलान्तमुत्पपात नभो हरिः

sa lilaṅghuyirbhīmam salīlam lavaṇārṇavam
kallolāsphālavelāntamutpapāta nabho hariḥ

With a desire to cross easily the terrific ocean, whose shores were being lashed by its
waves, which Hanuma jumped up into the air.

34. lilaN^ghayiSuH= with a desire to cross;saliilam= easily;bhiimam lavaNaarNavam= the terrific ocean;kallola sphaalavelaantam= whose shores were being lashed by its waves;saH hariH= that Hanuma;utpapaata= jumped up (into the sky).

Hanuma again visits Ashoka garden and sees Seetha once more. He consoles Seetha that Rama along with his forces of monkeys and bears will come soon and after conquering the enemies in battle, will take her back. After bidding adieu to Seetha, he ascends Mount Arishta and enlarges his body making himself ready to leap across the ocean.
