The monkeys enter Madhuvana and partake the honey in plenty…

The monkeys enter Madhuvana and partake the honey in plenty, as permitted by Hanuma and Angada.

1Sundara Kanda 62.1

तान् उवाच हरि श्रेष्ठो हनूमान् वानर ऋषभः |
अव्यग्र मनसो यूयम् मधु सेवत वानराः
अहमावारयिष्यामि युष्माकम् परिपन्थिनः

tān uvāca hari śreṣṭho hanūmān vānara ṛṣabhaḥ |
avyagra manaso yūyam madhu sevata vānarāḥ
ahamāvārayiṣyāmi yuṣmākam paripanthinaḥ

Hanuma, like a bull among the monkeys and the foremost among them spoke to them as follows:
"Cherish the honey with a cool mind, O monkeys! I will keep off your opposer who is standing in your
way."

2Sundara Kanda 62.2

5Sundara Kanda 62.5

6Sundara Kanda 62.6

40Sundara Kanda 62.40

स दीन वदनो भूत्वा कृत्वा शिरसि च अञ्जलिम् |
सुग्रीवस्य शुभौ मूर्ध्ना चरणौ प्रत्यपीडयत्

sa dīna vadano bhūtvā kṛtvā śirasi ca añjalim |
sugrīvasya śubhau mūrdhnā caraṇau pratyapīḍayat

The source page does not carry a separate English translation for this shloka. The Devanagari and IAST are complete.

The monkeys enter Madhuvana and partake the honey in plenty, as permitted by Hanuma and Angada. Singing and dancing as if in a drunken state, the monkeys were obstructed by those guarding the grove. The terrific monkeys warded off the guards, nay, they even dragged them by their knees and tossed them up in the air. The guards then went to Dadhimukha, the chief of the garden-guards and complained him of the matter. Dadhimukha along with his followers appears on the scene and invade the monkeys with trees and rocks. Angada thrashed Dadhimukha with his arms and made him fall on the ground. Dadhimukha along with his followers then proceed to Kishkindha to report the matter to him. Dadhimukha bows down and touches Sugreeva's feet as a mark of salutation.
