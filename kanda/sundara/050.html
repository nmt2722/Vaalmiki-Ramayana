As instructed by Ravana, Prahasta his chief minister inquires of Hanuma as to who he was and also his motive in destroying the pleasure-garden as also killing the demons.

1Sundara Kanda 50.1

तम् उद्वीक्ष्य महाबाहुः पिन्ग अक्षम् पुरतः स्थितम् |
रोषेण महता आविष्टो रावणो लोक रावणः
स राजा रोष ताम्र अक्षः प्रहस्तम् मन्त्रि सत्तमम्

tam udvīkṣya mahābāhuḥ pinga akṣam purataḥ sthitam |
roṣeṇa mahatā āviṣṭo rāvaṇo loka rāvaṇaḥ
sa rājā roṣa tāmra akṣaḥ prahastam mantri sattamam

That Ravana, the long-armed, who caused people to cry, looking up at that monkey standing
in front of him, was affected with violent anger, having his mind seized with suspicion, speculated
(as follows) about Hanuma, the jewel among the monkeys, who was invested with splendour.

10Sundara Kanda 50.10

19Sundara Kanda 50.19

दूतोऽहमिति विज्ञेयो राघवस्यामितौजसः |
श्रूयताम् चापि वचनम् मम पथ्यमिदम् प्रभो

dūto.ahamiti vijñeyo rāghavasyāmitaujasaḥ |
śrūyatām cāpi vacanam mama pathyamidam prabho

"O, Lord of demons! Know me to be a messenger of Rama who is possessing I am unlimited
splendour. I am telling you a beneficial word. Listen to it."

19. aham= I;vijNeyaH= am to be known;duutaaH iti= as a messenger;raaghavasya= of Rama;amitoujasaH= possessing an unlimited splendour;prabho= O Lord of demons!;idam= let this;pathyam= beneficial;mama vachaam= word of mine;shruujataam cha= be heard (by you).

As instructed by Ravana, Prahasta his chief minister inquires of Hanuma as to who he was and also his motive in destroying the pleasure-garden as also killing the demons. In reply, Hanuma says that he destroyed the pleasure-garden in order that he might be taken captive and dragged to the presence of Ravana, whom he was eager to see and was forced in self-defense to kill those who stood in his way. Finally, he declares himself to be a messenger of Rama and adds that, though he was incapable of being bound by a missile presided over by Brahma, he surrendered to it only in order to see Ravana in person.
