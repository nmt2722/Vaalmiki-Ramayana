Sundara Kanda · Sarga 68

Hanuma informs Rama how Seetha expressed her grave doubt as to how monkeys…

Hanuma informs Rama how Seetha expressed her grave doubt as to how monkeys and bears can leap across a vast sea, as well as her appeal to Rama to destroy Ravana along with his entire army in Lanka and take her back to Ayodhya.

1Sundara Kanda 68.1

अथ अहम् उत्तरम् देव्या पुनः उक्तः ससम्भ्रमम् |
तव स्नेहान् नर व्याघ्र सौहार्यात् अनुमान्य च

29Sundara Kanda 68.29

ततो मया वाग्भिः अदीन भाषिणी |
शिवाभिः इष्टाभिः अभिप्रसादिता |
जगाम शान्तिम् मम मैथिल आत्मजा |
तव अपि शोकेन तथा अभिपीडिता

tato mayā vāgbhiḥ adīna bhāṣiṇī |
śivābhiḥ iṣṭābhiḥ abhiprasāditā |
jagāma śāntim mama maithila ātmajā |
tava api śokena tathā abhipīḍitā

"Thereafter, soothened by me with comforting, well-disposed and agreeable words, Seetha,
though tormented with thought of your grief, obtained peace of mind."

29. tataH= thereafter;tava shokena api= though tormented with thought of your sorrow too;abhipiiDitaa= the tormented;maithila aatmajaa= Seetha;abhiprasaadita= was soothened;mayaa= by me;adiina bhaaShiNii= through comforting words spoken;mama vaagbhiH= and by my words;shiivaabhiH= which were well disposed;iSTaabhiH= and agreeable;jagaama= obtained;shaantim= peace of mind.

Hanuma informs Rama how Seetha expressed her grave doubt as to how monkeys and bears can leap across a vast sea, as well as her appeal to Rama to destroy Ravana along with his entire army in Lanka and take her back to Ayodhya. Hanuma further informs Rama, how he resolved her doubt by explaining the prowess of the troops of monkeys and bears, commanded by Sugreeva as also how he brought solace and peace to her from her worries.
