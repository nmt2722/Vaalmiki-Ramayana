How to read the counts. Mentions are counted on the Devanagari text and include common epithets, so Rama's count includes राघव and रघुनन्दन. Colophons and the Gayatri appendix are excluded. The coloured bar under each card shows where the mentions fall across the six kandas.

First appearances, in order

The index is sorted by presence in the text, not by importance. For the story's own order, follow the sarga links.

The first appearance is the earliest verse in the six kandas where the character's name or a standard epithet occurs. Because Narada summarises the whole story in Bala Kanda 1, many principal characters are first named there — the summary reaches Rama at verse 8, Sita at 27, Lakshmana at 25 and Ravana at 49.
